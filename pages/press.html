Short description

Never miss a return window. Every Item is an iPhone app that scans a receipt, fills in the store's usual return-by date and reminds you before it closes. It tracks warranties too, and a home inventory PDF is free. Everything stays on your iPhone. There's no account and no sign-up, and the free version has no item limit.

What's new in 1.0.2

Released November 13, 2026. Clearer App Store page and small fixes. Every Item stays free with no item limit and no account.

Long description

Never miss a return window again. Scan a receipt and Every Item fills in the store's usual return window, counts down the days and reminds you before it closes. Warranties, too.

No account. No sign-up. Free with no item limit. Your items stay on your iPhone.

Scan a receipt, skip the typing

Point your camera at a paper receipt. Every Item reads the store, date and total on your iPhone, nothing is uploaded, and fills in the store's usual return window for you to check. Always confirm the return policy with the store.

Never miss a return or warranty

  • See what's still returnable and what's due soon, at a glance
  • Get a reminder before a return window closes
  • Get a reminder before a warranty ends, while you can still claim
  • Change any date in a tap

Proof of purchase in one tap

Share a PDF with the receipt, photos, serial number and dates for a return or warranty claim. Free.

Your home, room by room

  • Add photos and organize everything you own by room
  • Quick add: photograph item after item and name them later
  • Emergency export: one tap makes a PDF of everything you own, with a photo of each item, room subtotals and a grand total. Free.
  • CSV export and backups are free too

Private by design

No account and no sign-in. Your items stay on your iPhone. Backups go only to a folder you choose, such as your own iCloud Drive. Optional App Lock with Face ID. Data Not Collected.

Every Item Pro

Everything above is free, with no item limit. Pro adds the customizable insurance report and the guided room walkthrough. Choose a one-time lifetime purchase or a yearly subscription. Both work with Family Sharing.

Key features

  • Scan paper receipts

    On-device text recognition reads them, and nothing is uploaded.

  • Return-deadline reminders

    A return-by countdown, with store return windows filled in for you.

  • Warranty-expiry alerts

    Arrive while there's still time to get a repair or replacement.

  • Photos and rooms

    Organize everything you own by room, with photos.
