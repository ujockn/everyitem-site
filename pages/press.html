Short description

Every Item is an iPhone app that saves your receipts, tracks warranties and reminds you before a return window closes. Everything stays on your iPhone. There's no account and no sign-up, and the free version has no item limit.

Key features

  • Scan paper receipts

    On-device text recognition reads them, and nothing is uploaded.

  • Return-deadline reminders

    A return-by countdown, with store return windows filled in for you.

  • Warranty-expiry alerts

    Arrive while there's still time to get a repair or replacement.

  • Photos and rooms

    Organize everything you own by room, with photos.

  • Free emergency insurance export

    An adjuster-ready PDF with photos and room totals. Never behind a paywall.

  • CSV, proof-of-purchase PDF and backups

    Exports and automatic backups are free.

  • Private by design

    Data stays on the device, protected by iOS Data Protection, with an optional Face ID lock.

  • Coming soon

    Import receipts from Mail, Files, Photos and the browser through the Share Sheet.
