Clothing goes on sale fast, and the windows to get the difference back are short. Shopbop only adjusts a price if the item is marked down **within 7 days of your order date**, according to its [Customer Service page](https://www.shopbop.com/ci/aboutShopBop/customerservice.html), and J.Crew also gives **7 days**. Gap Inc. brands and Kohl's give about two weeks, Nordstrom and Macy's give 10 days, and Nordstrom Rack gives none. This guide covers what each store's official page says about price adjustments after you buy, price matching other stores, what you need and how to ask. Every detail comes from official store pages, linked in [Sources](#sources).

**Last checked: Nov 9, 2026.** Every Item is an independent guide and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Nordstrom, Nordstrom Rack, Banana Republic, Gap, Old Navy, Athleta, Gap Inc., Shopbop, Macy's, Kohl's or J.Crew. Stores can change these rules at any time, so confirm on the official page before you rely on a date.

## Price adjustment or price match?

- **Price adjustment:** you already bought the item, the same store lowers its price, and you ask for the difference back. This is what most clothing stores offer.
- **Price match:** the store lowers its price to match another retailer, usually at the time you buy. Fewer clothing stores do this. Nordstrom says it doesn't, and Shopbop does for other online retailers at checkout.

For big-box stores like Target, Best Buy, Costco and Home Depot, see our [price adjustment policies guide](/guides/price-adjustment-policies/).

## Nordstrom

Nordstrom's [Pricing Policy](https://www.nordstrom.com/browse/customer-service/policy/pricing-policy) says that if Nordstrom lowers the price of an item you bought from it **at full price**, and the same color and size are still available, it will adjust the price when you ask **within 10 days** of your in-store purchase or shipment date.

**The rules:**

- Items you bought on sale aren't eligible.
- You get one price adjustment per item.
- Items from third-party sellers or Nordstrom Marketplace aren't eligible.
- Approved adjustments go back to your original form of payment.
- Nordstrom says it doesn't accept automated price adjustment requests from third-party services, so ask yourself.
- Items in Nordstrom savings events and the Nordstrom Anniversary Sale can qualify, as long as the same color and size are still available.

**Does Nordstrom price match?** No. The same page says Nordstrom stands behind its everyday pricing and doesn't price match other retailers. If you see the item cheaper somewhere else, Nordstrom's written policy won't cover it.

## Nordstrom Rack

Nordstrom Rack's [FAQ](https://www.nordstromrack.com/customer-service/frequently-asked-questions) says that Rack stores and nordstromrack.com don't price match or make price adjustments, because their prices change often. If an item you bought at the Rack drops in price, the only route is the return policy. Rack's FAQ says most purchases can be returned within 40 days, and eligible purchases made from Oct. 5 to Dec. 24, 2026 can be returned by Jan. 18, 2027 or 40 days after purchase, whichever is later. Final sale items can't be returned.

## Banana Republic, Gap, Old Navy and Athleta

All four Gap Inc. brands publish the same price adjustment rules on their own help pages: [Banana Republic](https://bananarepublic.gap.com/customerService/info.do?cid=80741&cs=price_adjustments), [Gap](https://www.gap.com/customer-service/getting-a-price-adjustment?cid=1192378), [Old Navy](https://oldnavy.gap.com/customerService/info.do?cid=83041&cs=price_adjustments) and [Athleta](https://athleta.gap.com/customerService/info.do?cid=3047330).

- **Window:** if something you bought goes down in price, you can ask for a one-time price adjustment **within 14 days of your order date**.
- **Promo codes:** if you used a promotion code or another discount on the purchase, it isn't eligible, unless the only discount was free shipping. Check the terms of the specific promotion.
- **Final sale** items aren't eligible.
- **Where to ask:** contact customer service by chat or phone, with your order number ready. It's on your shipping or order confirmation email. Each brand says price adjustments for online purchases **can't be done in a store**.
- **Refund:** if you qualify, the difference goes back to your original form of payment, such as your credit card.

Phone numbers listed on those pages (U.S.): Banana Republic 888-277-8953, Gap 800-427-7895, Athleta 877-328-4538. Banana Republic, Gap and Athleta list chat hours of 7 am to 1 am ET daily. The Old Navy page points to chat and phone from its Contact Us section.

The pages focus on online orders and don't describe how store purchases are handled, so call or ask your store before making a trip. The help pages I checked don't mention matching other retailers' prices.

## Shopbop

Shopbop's [Customer Service page](https://www.shopbop.com/ci/aboutShopBop/customerservice.html) says that if something you bought is **marked down within 7 days of the original order date**, Shopbop will adjust the price once.

- **How to ask:** call 1-877-746-7267 or email service@shopbop.com, and include your original order number.
- **Refund:** eligible refunds go back to your original form of payment.

**Price matching.** Shopbop also matches other online retailers' advertised prices, but only **at the time of purchase**. Call the same number to check that a match qualifies. The item has to be the identical style and color and first quality. The other retailer must be an authorized seller of the brand, show the price in U.S. dollars, and have the promotion visible on its site. Shopbop won't match auction, discount or liquidation sites, and during Shopbop promotions you get either the price match or the Shopbop promotion, not both.

## Macy's

Macy's [price adjustment page](https://www.macys.com/customer-service/articles/how-can-i-get-a-price-adjustment) says that if the price of an eligible item drops **within 10 days of your order date**, you can ask for the difference between what you paid and the new advertised Macy's price.

- **Online purchases:** call 1-800-289-6229, 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. ET, with your order number and item details.
- **Store purchases:** take your receipt to your local Macy's store.
- **Furniture and mattresses:** call 1-888-822-6229 within 10 days of the delivery date (White Glove, In-Home or Room of Choice delivery) or the ship date (warehouse or vendor shipments), with your salescheck number.
- **Catches:** promotional discounts can't be combined with a price adjustment, and certain merchandise, promotional offers, clearance items and other special purchases may not qualify. Refunds go to your original form of payment.

## Kohl's

Kohl's [price adjustment FAQ](https://www.kohls.com/faq/article/88) says you may qualify if you bought an item on sale or at regular price in a Kohl's store or on Kohls.com, its sale or regular price is now lower than what you paid **in the prior two weeks**, and you have the **original, dated sales receipt**.

- **Store purchases** have to be adjusted in a store. **Kohls.com purchases** are handled by contacting Kohl's Customer Service.
- **Not eligible:** clearance markdowns, Kohl's Cares merchandise, and items bought in a Buy One, Get One deal or currently in a BOGO promotion.
- **Coupons:** a coupon from your original purchase only applies to the adjustment if it's still valid.
- **Blackout periods:** Kohl's says it may decide that price adjustments aren't available during some periods. Ask before you count on one during a big sale.

## J.Crew

J.Crew's [Customer Service page](https://www.jcrew.com/help/customer-service) says it gives a one-time price adjustment on **full-price** merchandise **within 7 days** of the store purchase date or the mail-order ship date, if you have the original receipt. Anything not bought at full price is ineligible, orders placed with a coupon or promotion can't get extra discounts, and promotions and coupons can't be applied to earlier purchases.

## How to ask for a price adjustment

1. **Count the days right away.** Most of these windows count from the order or purchase date, not delivery. Nordstrom counts from the shipment date for online orders, and J.Crew from the ship date for mail orders.
2. **Check that you paid the kind of price that qualifies.** Nordstrom and J.Crew only adjust full-price purchases. Gap Inc. brands exclude purchases made with a promo code.
3. **Find the order number or receipt.** Shopbop, Macy's and the Gap Inc. brands ask for the order number. Kohl's wants the original, dated receipt, and J.Crew the original receipt.
4. **Use the right channel.** Online orders usually go through chat, phone or email. Gap Inc. brands say online purchases can't be adjusted in a store, and Kohl's says store purchases have to be adjusted in a store.
5. **Ask once, and clearly.** Several stores only allow one adjustment per item, so it can pay to wait until near the end of the window if you expect a deeper markdown, as long as you don't miss the deadline.

## If the window closed: return and rebuy

If you're past the adjustment window but still inside the return window, some shoppers return the item and buy it again at the lower price. That's not a store promise, just a workaround, and it only works if the item is unworn, still returnable and still at the lower price. Return windows for clothing are usually much longer than adjustment windows. Check the dates in our [holiday return deadlines](/guides/holiday-return-deadlines-2026/) guide, and if the return date has also passed, see [what you can still do after a return window closes](/guides/missed-return-window/).

## Keep the proof handy

Every store above asks for an order number or receipt, and the clock starts on the day you buy. Photograph store receipts at the register, keep order confirmation emails, and note the last day to ask. ## Frequently asked questions

### Does Nordstrom price match?

No. Nordstrom's Pricing Policy says it doesn't price match other retailers. It does adjust its own price if it lowers the price of an item you bought at full price, within 10 days of your in-store purchase or shipment date, as long as the same color and size are still available.

### How many days does Nordstrom give for a price adjustment?

10 days from your in-store purchase date or, for online orders, the shipment date. You get one adjustment per item, and items you bought on sale don't qualify.

### Does Nordstrom Rack do price adjustments?

No. Nordstrom Rack's FAQ says its stores and nordstromrack.com don't price match or make price adjustments. Your option is a return within the return window, if the item is eligible.

### Does Banana Republic do price adjustments?

Yes. Banana Republic gives a one-time price adjustment within 14 days of your order date. Purchases made with a promo code or other discount don't qualify, unless the only discount was free shipping, and final sale items are excluded. For online orders, contact Banana Republic by chat or phone with your order number. It says online purchases can't be adjusted in a store.

### Do Gap, Old Navy and Athleta have the same price adjustment policy as Banana Republic?

Yes. Each brand's help page lists the same rules: one adjustment within 14 days of the order date, no promo-code purchases except free shipping, no final sale items, and no in-store adjustments for online purchases.

### Does Shopbop do price adjustments?

Yes, once per item, if the item is marked down within 7 days of your original order date. Call 1-877-746-7267 or email service@shopbop.com with your order number. Shopbop also matches other online retailers' prices, but only at the time of purchase.

### Which clothing store gives the longest price adjustment window?

Of the stores in this guide, Gap, Banana Republic, Old Navy, Athleta and Kohl's give the most time, at 14 days or two weeks. Nordstrom and Macy's give 10 days, and Shopbop and J.Crew give 7.

### Can I get a price adjustment on an item I bought on sale?

It depends on the store. Nordstrom and J.Crew only adjust full-price purchases. Kohl's allows sale and regular-price items but excludes clearance. Gap Inc. brands exclude purchases made with a promo code or other discount, except free shipping.
