Buy a $100 Target GiftCard, keep the receipt, change your mind the next day, and you still can't get the $100 back. Target's [returns page](https://www.target.com/help/articles/returns-exchanges/returns) lists Target GiftCards and prepaid Visa, Mastercard and American Express gift cards as final sale, not returnable or redeemable for cash except where the law requires it. Walmart, Amazon, Best Buy and Costco say much the same. So the short answer is no: a receipt doesn't make a gift card returnable at these stores. This guide shows each store's rule, the legal exceptions (California's cash-back rule changed in 2026), and the jobs the receipt can still do for you. Every detail comes from official pages, linked in [Sources](#sources).

**Last checked: November 13, 2026.** Every Item is an independent guide and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Target, Walmart, Amazon, Best Buy or Costco. This is general information, not legal advice.

## Can you return a gift card? Store by store

| Store | Returnable with receipt? | What the store says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Target](#target) | No | Target GiftCards and prepaid Visa, Mastercard and Amex cards are final sale, except where law requires |
| [Walmart](#walmart) | No | Gift cards and branded Visa, Mastercard and Amex gift cards are final sale |
| [Amazon](#amazon) | No | No cash refund or cash redemption, except as required by law |
| [Best Buy](#best-buy) | No | Not redeemable for cash; third-party gift cards and prepaid cards can't be returned |
| [Costco](#costco) | No | Shop Cards and gift card items are non-refundable |

All five policies say the same thing, so the receipt won't get the card itself taken back. You have more room with the law, and with the things you buy *using* the card.

## Target

Target's [returns page](https://www.target.com/help/articles/returns-exchanges/returns) puts gift cards on its final sale list. That covers specialty gift cards, prepaid gift cards and Target GiftCards, including Visa, Mastercard and American Express gift cards and restaurant, entertainment and lottery cards. They can't be returned or redeemed for cash or credit except where the law requires it.

If you return an *item* you paid for with a Target GiftCard, the refund comes back as a **new Target eGiftCard**, not cash. If you paid with a Visa, Mastercard or Amex gift card, Target says the refund goes back onto that gift card, up to its original value, usually within 7 to 10 days. Full return windows are in our [Target return policy guide](/guides/target-return-policy/).

## Walmart

[Walmart's Return Policy](https://corporate.walmart.com/policies) (updated Nov. 2, 2026) lists **gift cards** among its final sale items, next to prepaid phone cards, prepaid gaming cards and SIM cards. Its financial services section separately lists **branded Visa, Mastercard and Amex gift cards** as final sale.

For purchases paid with a Walmart Gift Card, a receipted return goes back to a Walmart Gift Card or your Walmart Balance, in up to 3 hours. More on refund timing is in our [Walmart return policy guide](/guides/walmart-return-policy/).

## Amazon

Amazon's [Gift Card Terms and Conditions](https://www.amazon.com/gc-legal) say Amazon.com Gift Cards can't be redeemed for cash or returned for a cash refund, except to the extent the law requires. They also can't be resold, reloaded, or moved to another Amazon account. The same terms say Amazon isn't responsible if a card is lost, stolen or used without your permission, and that a card that doesn't work gets replaced, not refunded.

Two useful details: Amazon charges no fees on gift cards, and balances from cards issued after Oct. 1, 2005 don't expire. Amazon's [physical store return policy](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=202075110) also lists gift cards as not returnable, except as required by law. For everything else Amazon takes back, see our [Amazon return policy guide](/guides/amazon-return-policy/).

## Best Buy

Best Buy's [Gift Card Terms and Conditions](https://www.bestbuy.com/gcterms) say its gift cards have no expiration date and no fees, and are **not redeemable for cash**. Its [Return & Exchange Policy](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/help-topics/return-exchange-policy/pcmcat260800050014.c?id=pcmcat260800050014) separately lists codes and prepaid cards, including third-party gift cards, as items it won't take back.

This is where the receipt matters most. Best Buy replaces a **lost, stolen or damaged** gift card only with valid proof of purchase, and only for the balance left on the card. Its [gift card FAQ](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/gift-card-help/gift-card-faq/pcmcat1526048189330.c?id=pcmcat1526048189330) adds that if the card was bought at another retailer, you ask that retailer for the replacement. Returns of items bought with a Best Buy gift card are credited back to the card within 30 minutes of Best Buy receiving them. See our [Best Buy return policy guide](/guides/best-buy-return-policy/) for windows and fees.

## Costco

[Costco's return policy](https://customerservice.costco.com/app/answers/answer_view/a_id/1191) covers most merchandise with a satisfaction guarantee, but gift cards are an exception. It lists **Shop Cards** as non-refundable, and **gift card and ticket items** as non-refundable too. Our [Costco return policy guide](/guides/costco-return-policy/) covers the rest of the satisfaction guarantee.

## When the law lets you cash out a gift card

Store policies all say "except where required by law" for a reason. A few rules can work in your favor:

- **California: cash back under $15.** Since **April 1, 2026**, [California Civil Code section 1749.5](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=1749.5) says a gift certificate (which includes gift cards) with a cash value **under $15** is redeemable in cash. The old threshold was $10, and plenty of pages online still say $10. The same section bans expiration dates and most dormancy fees on gift certificates sold in California.
- **Federal law: at least five years.** The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau [explains](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/if-my-prepaid-card-expires-do-i-lose-my-money-en-423/) that gift card funds must be good for at least five years, and inactivity fees can only start after 12 months of no use. The rule itself is [12 CFR 1005.20](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-X/part-1005/subpart-A/section-1005.20). It doesn't make a card refundable, but it means an old card often still has value.
- **Other states** have their own gift card laws, and some go further than federal law, as the CFPB notes. Check your state attorney general's consumer pages before you assume a small balance is stuck.

None of this turns a $100 card back into $100 at the register. It mostly helps with leftover balances and old cards.

## What your gift card receipt is still good for

You can't return the card, but don't throw the receipt away:

1. **Replacing a lost or stolen card.** Best Buy only replaces one with proof of purchase. Other issuers may ask for the same.
2. **Reporting a scam.** If someone talked you into buying a gift card and reading them the number, contact the card's issuer right away with the card and the receipt, then report it at [ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov/). Best Buy's terms warn never to share a gift card number or PIN with someone you don't know.
3. **Proving the purchase date and amount** if the card never activated or shows the wrong balance.
4. **Returning things you buy with the card.** The refund usually goes back to a gift card (Target issues a new eGiftCard; Best Buy credits the original card), so keep that receipt with the item's receipt.

## Bought a gift card by mistake? What to do

- **Spend it on something you'd buy anyway**, like groceries or household basics at that store.
- **Give it to someone who shops there.** Amazon says no part of an Amazon balance can be moved to another account, so give the card before you apply it to yours.
- **In California, spend it down** below $15 and ask for the rest in cash.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you return a gift card if you have the receipt?

Not at Target, Walmart, Amazon, Best Buy or Costco. All five list gift cards as non-returnable or non-refundable, and the receipt doesn't change that. The only exceptions they mention are cases where the law requires a refund or cash-out.

### Can you return a Visa or Mastercard gift card?

Not at Target or Walmart. Both list prepaid Visa, Mastercard and American Express gift cards as final sale. If you return an item you paid for with one at Target, the refund goes back onto the gift card, up to its original value.

### Can I get cash back for a gift card in California?

Yes, if the balance is under $15. California Civil Code section 1749.5 makes gift cards with a cash value under $15 redeemable in cash, a threshold that rose from $10 on April 1, 2026.

### Do gift cards expire?

Under federal rules, gift card funds must last at least five years, and inactivity fees can't start until after 12 months without use. Amazon and Best Buy say their cards have no expiration date and no fees, and California bans expiration dates on gift cards sold there.

### What happens if I return something I bought with a gift card?

You usually get the refund on a gift card, not cash. Target issues a new Target eGiftCard, Best Buy credits the original card within 30 minutes of receiving the return, and Walmart refunds to a Walmart Gift Card or Walmart Balance.

### Can a lost or stolen gift card be replaced?

Sometimes, if you have the receipt. Best Buy replaces lost, stolen or damaged cards only with valid proof of purchase, for the remaining balance. Amazon's terms say it isn't responsible for lost or stolen cards.

### I bought a gift card for a scammer. Can I get my money back?

Contact the gift card's issuer right away with the card and your receipt, and ask whether the funds can be frozen or refunded. Then report it at ReportFraud.ftc.gov. Amazon's own terms warn that many scams ask to be paid by gift card.
