A price drop right after you buy can cost you real money, and whether you get it back depends on the store. Costco and Home Depot give you **30 days** to ask, Target gives you **14 days** (longer for holiday purchases), Best Buy ties it to your return window, and Amazon and Walmart.com don't promise anything for items you already bought. This guide covers what each store's official page says about refunding the difference after a price drop, what proof you need and how to ask. Every detail comes from official store and card issuer pages, linked in [Sources](#sources).

**Last checked: Nov 4, 2026.** Every Item is an independent guide and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Amazon, Target, Walmart, Best Buy, Costco, The Home Depot, Lowe's or Chase. Stores can change these rules at any time, so confirm on the official page before you rely on a date.

## Price adjustment, price match, price protection: what's the difference?

Stores use these words loosely, so it helps to split them up.

- **Price adjustment (after purchase):** you already bought the item, the same store later lowers its price, and you ask for the difference back. This is what most people mean after Black Friday.
- **Price match (before purchase):** the store matches a lower price, often a competitor's, when you check out. Several stores say this does not apply to Black Friday deals and other special events.
- **Price protection:** a credit card benefit, covered near the end of this guide.

This guide is about the first one, plus the competitor rules where a store's own page mentions them.

## Target

Target's [price match policy](https://www.target.com/help/article/000062256) lets you ask for a price match at checkout **or within 14 days after you buy**, if you find the identical item for less at Target.com, in Target stores or through a Target Circle deal. It compares Target to Target only. The official page lists no other store as a match source.

**Holiday 2026 extension.** Starting Nov. 1, 2026, if you buy in store or online and the price at Target goes lower on or before Dec. 24, 2026, you can request a price match, according to Target's [Price Match Guarantee page](https://www.target.com/help/articles/policies-guidelines/price-match-guarantee). Before Nov. 1 or after Dec. 24, the normal 14 days apply. The page doesn't give a separate last day to ask for the holiday period, so don't wait.

**What you need:**

- The original receipt (paper or digital) or the packing slip. Target says one of these is required for price adjustments up to 14 days after purchase.
- Your order number and item details for online orders.
- The current lower price shown live on Target.com, in the Target app or in Target's digital ad. **Screenshots and pictures can't be accepted.** For the app, it must be showing your current store's price.

**The item must be identical** in brand, size, weight, color, quantity and model number. An item doesn't have to be in stock, but Target doesn't give rain checks for price match requests.

**How to ask:**

1. In store, take your receipt to Guest Services and show the lower price on your phone.
2. For Target.com or Target app orders, chat with Target online or call Guest Services at 1-800-591-3869.

**What's excluded:** clearance, closeout and liquidation items, refurbished, used, open-package and pre-owned items, pricing typos, pre-order items, contract phones, and a lower price at a different Target store. Target coupons, Target Circle Bonuses and gift card offers can't be combined with a price match. Target Plus items sold by partners have their own price match rules, and the lower price has to be on Target.com. Target.com price matches aren't available in Alaska and Hawaii.

## Best Buy

Best Buy's [Price Match Guarantee page](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/price-match-guarantee/best-buy-price-match-guarantee/pcmcat297300050000.c?id=pcmcat297300050000) says that if Best Buy lowers its in-store, online or app price during your return and exchange period, it will match the lower price on request. Best Buy's standard return and exchange period is [15 days for most products, or 60 days for My Best Buy Plus and Total members](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/help-topics/return-exchange-policy/pcmcat260800050014.c?id=pcmcat260800050014).

**Holiday 2026.** Most products sold and shipped by Best Buy between Oct. 30 and Dec. 31, 2026 can be price matched through Jan. 15, 2027. The same exclusions as the extended holiday return period apply, for example items bought with a third-party contract (cell phones, AppleCare+ plans) and holiday decorations. Best Buy's [holiday announcement](https://corporate.bestbuy.com/2026/holiday-calendar/) says that if the price of a product sold by Best Buy drops during the holiday season, it will match it.

**A Black Friday warning.** Best Buy's page lists "items denoted as Black Friday pricing" and items for sale from the Thursday before Thanksgiving through the Monday after under its competitor offers. That wording is about other stores' prices. If you're refused on one of Best Buy's own drops during Thanksgiving week, ask the person helping you to point to the exact wording.

**How to ask:**

- **BestBuy.com orders** (including store pickup and orders from the app or the kiosk outside a store): use online chat or call 1-888-BEST BUY (1-888-237-8289). Best Buy says these requests can't be done in stores or by email. Chat requests must come from the person who bought. You can check eligibility on your Order Details page with the "See details" link.
- **Purchases made in a store:** talk to a Customer Specialist or go to the Customer Service desk.

**More details:**

- You can price match more than once during the return and exchange period, if the other criteria are met.
- If Best Buy drops its price before an item scheduled for delivery arrives, it says it refunds 100% of the difference, including tax. You must call 1-888-BEST BUY for that.
- You can use a coupon or a price match, but not both.
- Not covered: Marketplace products, clearance, open-box, refurbished and pre-owned items, items in bundles, financing offers, and limited-quantity items.

## Costco

Costco's [price match page](https://customerservice.costco.com/app/answers/answer_view/a_id/628) (published May 15, 2026) says purchases that go down in price **within 30 days of the purchase date** are eligible for a price match. It covers Costco's own price drops only. Costco does not match other businesses, and does not match warehouse prices for Costco.com purchases.

**Sale-dated promotions.** For items with specific sale dates, such as Holiday Savings promotions, you must ask within both the promotion dates and the 30 days after you bought. Costco says requests made after the promotion ends may be denied. In practice, ask while the sale is still running.

**How to ask:**

- **Costco.com orders:** use the Request a Price Match button on that Costco page and fill in the form. Once approved, Costco says credits typically go to your original form of payment within 5 to 10 business days.
- **Warehouse purchases:** ask at the Returns counter at the warehouse where you bought the item.

Costco says it can deny a price match at its sole discretion, may restrict the policy in the future, and doesn't price match precious metals. The page doesn't list what proof to bring, so take your receipt or order number.

## The Home Depot

The Home Depot's [price match page](https://www.homedepot.com/c/price-match-and-price-check) says that if the price of an item you bought drops within **30 days of the purchase date**, you can ask for the difference, in store or online. Home Depot says this applies to a lower price on homedepot.com or at a competitor it matches.

**What you need:** your order number, receipt or other proof of purchase, plus evidence of the lower price, such as a screenshot or link. Contact customer service by phone (online orders: 1-800-430-3376) or live chat, or visit a store. Home Depot says refunds usually go to the original payment method.

**What's excluded:** clearance items, special orders, and products purchased during promotional events. Separately, Home Depot says it doesn't price match Black Friday or other limited-time promotions. Coupons and promo codes can't be combined with a price match.

## Lowe's

Lowe's [returns page](https://www.lowes.com/l/help/returns-policy) answers the question directly: if you find a lower price after you buy, chat with Lowe's or call 1-877-465-6937, and they'll review your purchase and the lower price to decide if it qualifies for a price adjustment. Only the person who made the purchase can ask. The official pages don't state a time limit, and they describe a competitor's lower price on an identical, in-stock item that can ship to you.

The [Lowest Price Guarantee page](https://www.lowes.com/l/about/lowest-price-guarantee) excludes clearance, seasonal and closeout items, third-party marketplace sellers, and one-time promotions such as Black Friday and other holiday promotions. It also can't be combined with other Lowe's offers or coupons. Because Lowe's pages don't say whether its own price drops are covered, ask before assuming.

## Walmart

Walmart's [price match policy](https://corporate.walmart.com/policies) (updated June 8, 2023) is about the moment of checkout. In a Walmart store, the customer has to tell the associate the price at checkout, and Walmart matches the identical item's price on Walmart.com. It excludes Walmart.com prices on special events, including clearance, Rollback, Black Friday and Cyber Monday deals, and it excludes Marketplace sellers. The store manager has the final say.

For Walmart.com orders, Walmart says it doesn't offer price matching for "prices for items previously purchased on Walmart.com that have since decreased in price" (see also [Ask Walmart](https://corporate.walmart.com/askwalmart/does-walmart-price-match)). I found no published Walmart policy that refunds the difference after a store purchase either. No published price adjustment policy found as of Nov 4, 2026.

## Amazon

Amazon's [Pricing and Price Matching page](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=TDZ7rEMCXwi0eZtpmR) says Amazon doesn't offer price matching compensation. I found no Amazon help page that promises a refund when the price of a released item drops after you buy it. No published price adjustment policy found as of Nov 4, 2026.

The one written guarantee is the [Pre-order Price Guarantee](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=TNPbc4KswePhOlODdm). It only covers not-yet-released items that show the Pre-order Price Guarantee message on the product page, and only items sold by Amazon.com. For those, you're charged the lowest Amazon.com price between ordering and the end of the release date.

## Can't get an adjustment? Return and rebuy

Where there's no price adjustment, some shoppers return the item and buy it again at the lower price. That's not a store promise, just a workaround, and it only works if the item is still returnable, you're inside the window, and the lower price is still there. Check the window first in our [Amazon](/guides/amazon-return-policy/) and [Walmart](/guides/walmart-return-policy/) return policy guides, and the [holiday return deadlines](/guides/holiday-return-deadlines-2026/), which list the extended holiday windows. If you've opened the item, read [Can You Return Opened Items?](/guides/can-you-return-opened-items/) first.

## What about credit card price protection?

Some credit cards used to refund the difference when a price dropped after you bought. It's mostly gone. Chase says on its official page that its credit cards currently don't come with a price protection benefit. I couldn't confirm from an official issuer page that any specific card still offers it in 2026, so I'm not naming one. Check your card's guide to benefits, or call the number on the back of your card, before counting on it. If your card does have it, expect to file a claim with your statement, an itemized receipt and proof of the lower price.

## How to ask for a price adjustment

1. **Check the date.** Count the days from your purchase date, not from delivery, and compare with the store's window above.
2. **Confirm it's the identical item:** same brand, model number, size and color.
3. **Gather proof of purchase:** receipt, packing slip or order number.
4. **Get proof of the lower price,** the way that store accepts it. Target wants the live price on Target.com or its app, not a screenshot. Home Depot accepts a screenshot or link.
5. **Ask the right place:** store service desk or Returns counter, online chat, or the phone number on the store's page.
6. **Skip stacked offers.** Several stores say you can't combine a price match with coupons or promo codes.

## How to never lose the proof

Every store above asks for proof of purchase, and every deadline counts from the day you bought. A few habits help:

- **Photograph the receipt the day you buy.** Keep order confirmation emails too.
- **Write the purchase date and the store's window next to the receipt photo.**
- **Check the item's price again** a few days after a big purchase, and before the window closes.
- **Keep your receipts long enough** to cover returns and warranties. See [How Long to Keep Receipts](/guides/how-long-to-keep-receipts/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Will Target refund the difference if the price drops after I buy?

Yes, if Target's own price on the identical item drops. Target says you can ask within 14 days of purchase, and for purchases made from Nov. 1, 2026, a price drop on or before Dec. 24, 2026 qualifies. You need your receipt or packing slip and the live lower price on Target.com or the Target app, since screenshots aren't accepted.

### Does Best Buy do price adjustments on Black Friday purchases?

Best Buy says that if its own price drops during your return and exchange period, it will match the lower price on request. For most purchases from Oct. 30 to Dec. 31, 2026, that means through Jan. 15, 2027. Its exclusion list mentions Black Friday pricing and the days around Thanksgiving under competitor offers, so if you hit trouble, ask the representative to show the wording.

### Does Amazon refund the difference if the price goes down?

Amazon's help pages don't promise it for items that have already been released. Its pricing page says it doesn't offer price matching compensation. The only written guarantee is the Pre-order Price Guarantee for qualifying items that aren't out yet.

### Does Walmart adjust the price after I buy?

I couldn't find a Walmart policy that does. Walmart.com says it doesn't price match items you already bought that later dropped in price, and the store price match policy is about the price at checkout.

### How many days does Costco give for a price adjustment?

Costco says purchases that drop in price within 30 days of the purchase date are eligible. For items on a dated promotion, you also have to ask while the promotion is still active. Ask at the Returns counter for warehouse purchases, or use the form for Costco.com orders.

### Does Home Depot refund a price drop after purchase?

Yes, within 30 days of the purchase date, in store or online. You need your order number or receipt and evidence of the lower price. Clearance items, special orders and products bought during promotional events are excluded.

### Do I need my receipt?

For every store above, expect to prove the purchase. Target requires a receipt or packing slip, and Home Depot asks for an order number, receipt or other proof. Costco's page doesn't list a proof requirement, so bring a receipt or your order number anyway.

### Do credit cards still refund price drops?

Mostly not. Chase says its cards don't currently offer price protection, and I couldn't verify from an official issuer page that a specific card still does in 2026. Look in your card's guide to benefits.
