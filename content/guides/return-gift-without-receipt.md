Return a Best Buy gift with no proof of purchase and Best Buy says you get **store credit**, the return **may be denied or limited**, and **sales tax and fees will not be reimbursed**, per its [Return & Exchange Policy](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/help-topics/return-exchange-policy/pcmcat260800050014.c?id=pcmcat260800050014). At Target you may get a merchandise return card instead of cash, and at Walmart a non-receipted return needs a government photo ID. This guide covers gift returns with and without a gift receipt at Target, Walmart, Amazon, Best Buy and Costco: what you get back, what ID is required and how to improve your odds. ## Target

[Target's returns page](https://www.target.com/help/articles/returns-exchanges/returns) treats gift returns differently depending on what you bring:

- **With a gift receipt or packing slip:** you get a **Target GiftCard**. In-store returns that use a gift receipt **cannot be processed on the same day the gift was purchased**.
- **Without a gift receipt or packing slip:** Target says you **may** get a **merchandise return card** in store.
- **[Merchandise return card](https://www.target.com/help/article/000062592):** Target issues these for non-receipted Target product returns in store. You need a **government-issued ID**. The card is redeemable **only in Target stores** (not on Target.com), is not cash or credit except where the law requires otherwise, is not transferable, and you may need the same ID to redeem it.
- **Opened, damaged or no-receipt items** may be denied a refund or exchange. Returns and exchanges without a receipt may also be limited.
- **Target Plus Partner items:** you must present a receipt or the original payment method for a lookup. In-store returns without an eligible receipt are denied.

Holiday gifts get extra time on some categories. Target's [Holiday FAQs](https://www.target.com/help/articles/holiday-help/holiday-help-faqs) say electronics and entertainment bought Nov. 1 through Dec. 24, 2026 are returnable through **Jan. 24, 2027** (from Dec. 26 onward for that window), and Apple, Beats and unlocked or prepaid phones through **Jan. 8, 2027**. Full dates are in our [holiday return deadlines for 2026](/guides/holiday-return-deadlines-2026/) guide.

## Walmart

[Walmart's Return Policy](https://corporate.walmart.com/policies) (updated Nov. 2, 2026) gives most items **90 days** after purchase or upon receipt. For gifts, Walmart spells out two paths:

- **With a gift receipt:** the item can be **exchanged**, refunded to a **Walmart Gift Card**, or **credited to the sender's original method of payment**.
- **Without a gift receipt:** it can be **exchanged** or refunded to a **Walmart Gift Card**.

**No receipt at all** (not even a gift receipt) falls under Walmart's non-receipted returns rules:

- You must present a **valid government-issued photo ID**.
- Walmart stores ID information to authorize returns.
- If the refund verification process accepts the return, you get a **Walmart gift card**.
- Walmart **limits the number and frequency** of non-receipted returns.

Walmart can also try to look up a store purchase with the debit or credit card used, or an online order with a phone number or email.

**Holiday window:** most items bought **Oct. 1 through Dec. 31** are returnable until **Jan. 31**. Exceptions can include wireless phones, protection plans, major appliances, Dyson products and select seasonal and luxury goods.

## Amazon

Amazon's [Return Policy](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GKM69DUUYKQWKWX7) has a dedicated **Returning a Gift** section:

1. Go to Amazon's Returns page.
2. Enter the **17-digit order number** from the packing slip that came with the item, or from the digital gift receipt (format like `123-1234567-1234567`).
3. If you can't find the order number, **ask the gift giver**.
4. For an **exchange or replacement**, return the gift and place a new order.

Kindle books received as a gift can be exchanged for an Amazon.com Gift Card before you accept them. For physical gifts, Amazon's gift section focuses on starting the return with the order number rather than promising cash.

If the gift was opened or is not in original condition, Amazon may charge a **damage fee of up to 50%** of the item price (100% for Amazon Luxury). See our [Amazon return damage fee guide](/guides/amazon-return-damage-fee/).

**Holiday 2026:** most items bought Nov. 1 through Dec. 31, 2026 can go back through **Jan. 31, 2027**. Apple-branded products in that purchase window can go back through **Jan. 15, 2027**.

## Best Buy

[Best Buy's Return & Exchange Policy](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/help-topics/return-exchange-policy/pcmcat260800050014.c?id=pcmcat260800050014) covers gifts explicitly:

- **Gift returned in a Best Buy store** (Best Buy purchase): you get **store credit for the purchase price**.
- **Gift returned by mail:** Best Buy credits the **gift purchaser** and emails them.
- **Marketplace gifts:** the gift purchaser is credited.

**Without proof of purchase**, Best Buy says returns:

- may require an **email address**,
- are refunded as **store credit**,
- **may be denied or limited**, and
- **will not reimburse state sales taxes and fees**.

Restocking fees still apply when the policy says they do. Opened digital cameras, camera lenses, camcorders, drones and projectors carry a **15%** restocking fee; opened activatable devices can carry a **$45** fee. Those fees still apply in the holiday period. For opened-item rules more broadly, see [can you return opened items](/guides/can-you-return-opened-items/).

**Windows:** most products have **15 days** from the day you receive them (**60** for My Best Buy Plus and Total members). Holiday: most purchases made **Oct. 30 through Dec. 31, 2026** can be returned through **Jan. 15, 2027** (exclusions apply, including some activatable devices and holiday decorations).

## Costco

Costco's pages don't describe a separate "gift return" process the way Target or Walmart do. What they do say:

- [What is Costco's return policy?](https://customerservice.costco.com/app/answers/answer_view/a_id/1191) describes a **satisfaction guarantee**, with **electronics** (TVs, computers, tablets, phones, cameras, major appliances and related categories) returnable within **90 days from the date the member received the merchandise**.
- [How can I return an item purchased in a Costco warehouse?](https://customerservice.costco.com/app/answers/answer_view/a_id/712) says a **receipt or the original packaging helps but may not be necessary**, and that limitations apply.

You'll need an active Costco membership to return in a warehouse. Costco's return policy does not list a holiday extension, so electronics gifts still follow the 90-day rule from when the item was received. Ask at the returns counter if you have packaging but no receipt.

## Practical tips before you go

1. **Ask the giver for a gift receipt, packing slip, order number or the card used.** That single ask is the difference between a GiftCard and a limited no-proof return at most of these stores.
2. **Keep the box unopened until you're sure.** Opened gifts can be denied at Target, can trigger Amazon damage fees, and can trigger Best Buy restocking fees on cameras and some devices. See [returning opened items](/guides/can-you-return-opened-items/).
3. **Check the holiday deadline for the purchase date, not the unwrapping date.** Our [holiday return deadlines for 2026](/guides/holiday-return-deadlines-2026/) guide lists each store's purchase window and last day.
4. **Bring government photo ID** if you have no receipt at Walmart or Target (merchandise return card), and an email address if Best Buy asks for one.
5. **If you already missed the window**, see [what you can still do after a missed return window](/guides/missed-return-window/).
6. **Keep receipts longer than you think.** A quick photo of a gift receipt helps later. See [how long to keep receipts](/guides/how-long-to-keep-receipts/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you return a gift without a receipt?

Often, but usually as store credit or a gift card, not cash, and the store may limit or deny it. Target may issue a merchandise return card, Walmart needs a government photo ID and pays a Walmart gift card if verification accepts, Best Buy may deny or limit no-proof returns and will not reimburse sales tax and fees, and Costco says a receipt helps but may not be necessary.

### What do you get back for a Target gift return?

With a gift receipt or packing slip, a Target GiftCard. Without one, you may get a merchandise return card in store. That card needs a government-issued ID, works only in Target stores (not Target.com), and is not cash except where the law requires it.

### Does Walmart need ID to return a gift with no receipt?

Yes for non-receipted returns. Walmart requires a valid government-issued photo ID, stores that ID information to authorize returns, and issues a Walmart gift card if verification accepts. It also limits how many non-receipted returns you can make and how often.

### How do you return an Amazon gift?

Use the 17-digit order number from the packing slip or digital gift receipt on Amazon's Returns page. If you can't find it, ask the gift giver. For a different size or item, return the gift and place a new order.

### Does Best Buy refund sales tax on a gift with no receipt?

No. Best Buy says returns lacking proof of purchase are store credit, may be denied or limited, and state sales taxes and fees will not be reimbursed. Restocking fees on opened cameras and some devices can still apply.

### Can Costco take a gift back without a receipt?

Costco says a receipt or the original packaging helps but may not be necessary, and limitations apply. Electronics still have a 90-day window from when the member received the item. Bring your membership card and any packaging you have.

### What is the last day to return holiday gifts in 2026?

It depends on the store and when the item was bought. Walmart and Amazon list most holiday purchases returnable through Jan. 31, 2027; Best Buy through Jan. 15, 2027 for most items; Target electronics through Jan. 24, 2027 (Apple/Beats/phones earlier). See [holiday return deadlines for 2026](/guides/holiday-return-deadlines-2026/).

### Should I open a gift before deciding to return it?

Wait if you can. Target says opened or damaged items may be denied, Amazon may charge a damage fee of up to 50% if the item is not in original condition, and Best Buy charges restocking fees on opened cameras and some other products. More detail is in [can you return opened items](/guides/can-you-return-opened-items/).
