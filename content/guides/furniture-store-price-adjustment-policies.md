Furniture often goes on sale while an order is still waiting to ship, and the difference can be large. West Elm's official [returns page](https://www.westelm.com/customer-service/return/) says prior purchases are not eligible for a price adjustment, even if the item has not shipped yet, and that shipping and processing fees are not refunded. This guide covers what West Elm, Pottery Barn, Williams Sonoma, Wayfair, Crate & Barrel and IKEA publish about refunding a price drop after you buy, matching a competitor when you check out, and the catches if you try to return the item and buy it again. Every detail comes from official store pages, linked in [Sources](#sources).

**Last checked: Nov 10, 2026.** Every Item is an independent guide and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by West Elm, Pottery Barn, Williams Sonoma, Williams-Sonoma Inc., Wayfair, Crate & Barrel or IKEA. Stores can change these rules at any time, so confirm on the official page before you rely on a date.

## Price adjustment or price match?

- **Price adjustment:** you already bought the item, the same store later lowers its price, and you ask for the difference back.
- **Price match:** the store matches a lower price, often a competitor's, usually at the time you buy.

For Target, Best Buy, Costco, Home Depot, Lowe's, Amazon and Walmart, see our [price adjustment policies guide](/guides/price-adjustment-policies/). For clothing and department stores, see our [clothing store price adjustment guide](/guides/clothing-store-price-adjustment-policies/).

## West Elm

West Elm's [returns page](https://www.westelm.com/customer-service/return/) has a clear rule: prior purchases are not eligible for price adjustments, **regardless of whether the item has yet to ship**. A sale that starts after you order, even before delivery, is not a promised credit under that policy.

West Elm's [Help Topics](https://www.westelm.com/customer-service/faq.html) also say prices can change, including temporary reductions, and that the price in your cart is the price you will be charged.

**If you want to cancel and reorder** before it ships, the same Help Topics page says to call Customer Service at 1-888-922-4119. Front Door deliveries cannot be canceled, because they are processed immediately, and West Elm says it cannot accept changes or cancellations on some items. Canceling is not the same as a price adjustment, and it is not guaranteed.

**If you try to return and rebuy,** these official limits matter:

- Eligible items can be returned within **30 days of receiving** the order, or **7 days** for Quick Ship upholstery. The clock starts when you receive the item, not when you order it.
- Made-to-order items, monogrammed items, mattresses and foundations, gift cards, open-box and final-sale items (prices ending in $.97), and items damaged through normal wear and tear are not eligible.
- Shipping and processing fees are not refunded. For mailed returns, you may also have to pay return shipping and processing costs, depending on the reason for the return.
- Most non-furniture items can be returned to a West Elm store for free. West Elm Outlets do not accept returns of any kind. Furniture and other large items have to be arranged by phone at 1-888-922-4119.
- Proof of purchase is required. West Elm accepts an original paper or gift receipt, an electronic receipt, a packing slip, an order number or a gift registry number.

Registry purchases have a longer window: 90 days from your event or 90 days from purchase, whichever is later, for eligible items.

## Pottery Barn

Pottery Barn's [returns page](https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/return/) uses the same after-purchase rule: prior purchases are not eligible for price adjustments. It also says delivery fees are not refunded.

The return windows match West Elm's for stocked items: **30 days** from receiving the order, or **7 days** for Quick Ship upholstery, with an original receipt or gift receipt. Final-sale items ending in .97 cannot be returned. Monogrammed items, made-to-order furniture or rugs, special-order items, custom upholstery, mattresses, gift cards and items damaged through normal wear and tear are not eligible.

Non-furniture items can be returned to a Pottery Barn store. Furniture returns are arranged by calling Customer Care at 1-888-779-5176. I found no Pottery Barn page that promises to match another store's price.

## Williams Sonoma

Williams Sonoma's [returns page](https://www.williams-sonoma.com/customer-service/return/) also says prior purchases are not eligible for price adjustments, and that delivery fees are not refunded. Most items with proof of purchase can be returned within **30 days**, and they must be in new condition. Final-sale items ending in .97, monogrammed or personalized items, custom upholstery, made-to-order items, some special-order items, perishable food, mattresses, outdoor grills and gift cards are not eligible. Online Williams Sonoma Home purchases can no longer be returned in Williams Sonoma retail stores or outlets.

**Price matching at checkout.** Separately, Williams Sonoma's [Price Match Promise](https://www.williams-sonoma.com/customer-service/price-matching-policy/) is only for **the day of your purchase**, in the U.S. It matches new products sold directly by Amazon (not third-party sellers), Sur la Table, Bloomingdale's, Macy's and Crate & Barrel, and items sold directly by the vendor on the vendor's own site. The item has to be identical in brand, size, weight, color, quantity and model number, in stock, at a published, valid price, and you can only get one match per identical item.

For an online order, email a copy of the current ad or a link to pricematch@wsgc.com **before** you buy, or call 1-877-812-6235. In a store, ask an associate. The page lists many exclusions, including clearance, limited-time and limited-quantity offers, coupons, storewide discounts, login-only prices, paid membership prices, private-label items, bundles, financing offers and shipping or delivery deals. It also says the promise is not intended to supersede Williams Sonoma's price adjustment policy. The returns page is the policy I found for purchases you already made, and it does not offer an adjustment.

## Wayfair

Wayfair's [Price Matching help article](https://www.wayfair.com/help/article/price_matching/8CBFFA0A-20D3-4D49-AC2D-65532EF3D2DB) is direct: it does not match competitors' prices, and it does not price match its own later price drops. If Wayfair puts an item on sale after you order, that page is not a promise that you will get the difference back.

## Crate & Barrel

Crate & Barrel's [Price Match Guarantee](https://www.crateandbarrel.com/price-guarantee) is also limited to **the day of your original purchase**, for online and in-store orders in the U.S. If you find a lower published, valid price on an identical name-brand item that is in stock at a listed competitor, Crate & Barrel says it will match that price when you ask, once per identical item per person. You need the ad, an online validation or the website address, and Crate & Barrel can verify the price and stock.

The U.S. competitor list on that page is Bed Bath & Beyond, Bloomingdale's, Macy's, Sur la Table, Pottery Barn, Target, Williams Sonoma, and Amazon for items fulfilled directly by Amazon (not Amazon Marketplace). Exclusions include clearance, closeout, limited-time and limited-quantity items, open-box, refurbished or damaged goods, category-wide or storewide discounts, coupon prices, login-only prices, paid membership prices, private-label items, bundles, financing, typographical errors and competitors' shipping or delivery fees.

That official page does not describe a price adjustment after the day you buy. I found no published post-purchase window on it, so don't count on a later markdown being credited unless Crate & Barrel confirms it for your order.

## IKEA

IKEA's [price changes page](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/customer-service/knowledge/articles/g311d1g8-bd93-4gdf-b149-gf467ge51f29.html) says that if an item you bought goes on sale or the price is reduced, you may be eligible for a price adjustment. It also says most IKEA promotions are not applicable to previous sales, and that an adjustment may be offered if some criteria are met. The page does not list those criteria or a time limit, so ask IKEA about your item rather than assuming a refund.

## If there's no adjustment: return and rebuy, or cancel

Where a store won't adjust the price, some shoppers return the item and buy it again at the lower price, or try to cancel an unshipped order and place a new one. That is not a store promise. It only works if the item is still returnable or cancelable, you're inside the window, and the lower price is still there.

On these furniture orders, the workaround is often more expensive than it looks:

- **Delivery fees may not come back.** West Elm says shipping and processing fees are not refunded. Pottery Barn and Williams Sonoma say the same about delivery fees. You can win the markdown and still lose the original delivery charge, and you may pay delivery again on the new order.
- **Some items can't go back at all.** Made-to-order and special-order furniture, monogrammed pieces, mattresses and final-sale prices ending in .97 are called out as ineligible at West Elm, Pottery Barn and/or Williams Sonoma.
- **The return clock can be short.** Quick Ship upholstery at West Elm and Pottery Barn has only 7 days from the day you receive it. West Elm Outlets accept no returns.
- **Canceling has limits.** West Elm says Front Door deliveries can't be canceled, and that some items can't be changed or canceled.

If the return window has also closed, see [what you can still do after a return window closes](/guides/missed-return-window/).

## Keep the proof handy

You'll need an order number or receipt to cancel, return or ask about a rare exception, and furniture return windows are often counted from the day the item arrives. Photograph store receipts, keep order confirmation emails, and note the delivery date and the last day to return. See [How Long to Keep Receipts](/guides/how-long-to-keep-receipts/) for how long to hold on to them, and [How to Make a Home Inventory for Insurance](/guides/home-inventory-for-insurance/) if the piece is staying.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does West Elm offer price adjustments?

No. West Elm's returns page says prior purchases are not eligible for price adjustments, even if the item has not shipped yet. A later sale is not a promised credit under that policy.

### What if West Elm drops the price before my order ships?

The written policy still says no adjustment. You can call 1-888-922-4119 and ask to cancel and reorder, but Front Door deliveries can't be canceled, some items can't be changed or canceled, and canceling is not guaranteed.

### Does Pottery Barn do price adjustments?

No. Pottery Barn's returns page says prior purchases are not eligible for price adjustments, and that delivery fees are not refunded. Eligible stocked items can still be returned within 30 days of receipt, or 7 days for Quick Ship upholstery.

### Does Williams Sonoma price match?

Yes, but only on the day you buy, and only against the competitors listed on its Price Match Promise page, such as Amazon when Amazon sells the item itself, Sur la Table, Bloomingdale's, Macy's, Crate & Barrel and the vendor's own site. After you buy, the returns page says prior purchases are not eligible for a price adjustment.

### Does Wayfair price match or refund a later sale price?

No. Wayfair's help article says it does not match competitors' prices and does not price match its own later price drops.

### Does Crate & Barrel match a lower price after I buy?

Crate & Barrel's official Price Match Guarantee applies only on the day of your original purchase, against a listed competitor's published, valid, in-stock price. That page does not publish a post-purchase adjustment window.

### Does IKEA give a price adjustment if something goes on sale?

It might. IKEA's price changes page says you may be eligible if some criteria are met, and that most promotions don't apply to earlier purchases. It doesn't list the criteria or a time limit, so ask IKEA about your item.

### Can I return furniture and buy it again at the lower price?

Sometimes, if the item is still returnable and the lower price is still there. That is not a promised adjustment. West Elm, Pottery Barn and Williams Sonoma say shipping or delivery fees are not refunded, and made-to-order, monogrammed, mattress and final-sale items often can't be returned at all.
