This guide explains Costco's return policy in plain English: what the satisfaction guarantee covers, which items have a 90-day limit, what you can't return, and how refunds work for online orders. Every detail comes from Costco's official customer service pages, linked in [Sources](#sources). Policies change, so check the official page before you rely on it.

**Last checked: Oct 22, 2026.** Every Item is an independent guide and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Costco.

## How long do you have to return to Costco?

[Costco's return policy](https://customerservice.costco.com/app/answers/answer_view/a_id/1191) is built around its **Risk-Free 100% Satisfaction Guarantee**. Costco says it guarantees your satisfaction on every product it sells and will refund your purchase price, with a list of exceptions. For most products, the policy **doesn't state a return deadline**. Costco adds that "limitations apply" and points members to the membership counter or Costco.com for details.

| Item | Return window or rule |
| --- | --- |
| Most products | No deadline listed; covered by the satisfaction guarantee (limitations apply) |
| TVs, projectors, computers, touchscreen tablets, smart wearables, cameras, drones, camcorders, MP3 players and cell phones | 90 days from the day you received it |
| Major appliances (refrigerators above 10 cu. ft., freezers, ranges, cooktops, over-the-range and under-counter microwaves, range hoods, dishwashers, water heaters, washers and dryers) | 90 days from the day you received it |
| Recreational carts ordered online | 90 days from the day you received it |
| Cell phones | 90 days; details vary by carrier service contract |
| Your Costco membership fee | Refundable at any time if you're dissatisfied |

**The 90-day clock starts when you receive the item**, not when you order it.

## Exceptions: what Costco won't refund

Costco's policy lists these exceptions:

- **Non-refundable:** airline and live performance event items, precious metals, Shop Cards, and gift card and ticket items.
- **Custom orders:** products made to your personal specifications through the Special Order Kiosk or Custom Installed Programs can't be returned or refunded, except for warranty repair or replacement if they don't meet the specifications.
- **Cigarettes and alcohol:** not accepted for return where prohibited by law.
- **Tires and batteries:** products with a limited useful life may be sold with their own limited warranty.
- **Diamonds of 1.00 carat or larger:** you must bring all the original paperwork (IGI and/or GIA certificates). You get a Jewelry Credit Memo, and a Costco Graduate Gemologist inspects the diamond for authenticity within 48 hours.

Some items follow separate instructions. Return **eyeglasses** bought in a warehouse to its Optical Department. For **hearing aids**, contact the Hearing Aid Center at your warehouse. **Contact lenses**, online eyeglasses and **large freight-delivered items** each have their own Costco help page.

## Returning to Costco without a receipt

Costco's help page on [warehouse returns](https://customerservice.costco.com/app/answers/answer_view/a_id/712/~/how-can-i-return-an-item-purchased-in-a-costco-warehouse) says it helps if you have **the receipt or original product packaging, but it may not be necessary** to process your return. Bring the item to any Costco warehouse, and the Member Services team will help.

Costco doesn't spell out on these pages how a no-receipt refund is paid, so ask at the returns counter.

## How to return something to Costco

1. **Warehouse purchases:** take the item to the returns counter at **any** Costco warehouse.
2. **Online purchases, in a warehouse:** you can return Costco.com orders to any warehouse for an **immediate refund, including shipping and handling fees**. If possible, the refund goes to the card you paid with, but accepted payment methods differ online and in the warehouse.
3. **Online purchases, by mail or pickup:** sign in on Costco.com, go to Orders & Returns, choose "Return or Replace Items" and follow the prompts. You'll get a return label (usually within the hour) or can schedule a pickup where eligible. Large items get emailed instructions.

Costco says items returned through Costco.com are refunded **including shipping and handling**, and you **won't be charged for return shipping**. Some items, such as food (including pet products), high-end jewelry, floral and paper products, need extra processing time.

## How long Costco online refunds take

From [Costco's refund timing page](https://customerservice.costco.com/app/answers/answer_view/a_id/1016980):

| How you return it | When the refund is typically issued |
| --- | --- |
| UPS return label | 2 to 3 business days after UPS scans the label |
| UPS Mail Innovations | 2 to 3 business days after the label is used |
| UPS pickup | 2 to 3 business days after UPS takes the item |
| Freight pickup | 2 business days after the carrier takes the item |

Some items need an extra **3 to 10 business days** for verification. After the refund is issued:

- **Credit or debit card:** it shows up according to your bank's policies.
- **Check:** it arrives in the mail within 15 business days.
- **Shop Card:** the card is reloaded within 48 hours, or a new Digital Shop Card is issued within 1 to 2 business days.

## Is there a holiday return window?

Costco's return policy doesn't mention a separate holiday window. Most products don't have a listed deadline anyway. **Holiday gifts in the electronics and appliance categories still follow the 90-day rule**, counted from when the item was received. A TV bought in early November and received right away will run out of time in early February.

## How to never miss a Costco return deadline

- **Keep receipts for electronics and appliances**, and note the 90-day date the day the item arrives.
- **Take a photo of the receipt.** Paper fades, and a photo helps even when a receipt isn't strictly required.
- **Keep the original box** for at least the first 90 days on big electronics.
- **Hold on to diamond certificates.** Without them, a return of a 1-carat-plus diamond gets complicated.
- **Set a reminder** a week before the 90-day mark so you have time to test and decide.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Costco have a time limit on returns?

For most products, Costco's policy doesn't list a deadline. Its satisfaction guarantee covers them, with limitations. TVs, computers, tablets, cameras, phones, major appliances and some other electronics must be returned within 90 days of when you received them.

### Can I return something to Costco without a receipt?

Costco says a receipt or the original packaging helps, but may not be necessary to process your return. Take the item to the returns counter at any Costco warehouse.

### Can I return a Costco.com order to a warehouse?

Yes. Returning an online order to any Costco warehouse gets you an immediate refund, including shipping and handling fees.

### How long does a Costco online refund take?

Typically 2 to 3 business days after UPS scans your label or picks up the item, plus 3 to 10 business days for some items that need verification. Then your bank's processing time applies.

### What can't you return to Costco?

Airline and live performance event items, precious metals, Shop Cards, gift card and ticket items, and custom-made products (except for warranty issues). Cigarettes and alcohol can't be returned where the law prohibits it.

### Can I get my Costco membership fee back?

Yes. Costco says it will cancel and refund your membership fee at any time if you're dissatisfied.
