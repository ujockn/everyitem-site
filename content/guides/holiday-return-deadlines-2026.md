A gift you buy in October can run out of return time before anyone opens it. Amazon, for example, says it may charge a **late fee of 20% of the item price** if you miss the "return by date", and 100% once 30 more days pass, per the [Amazon Return Policy](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GKM69DUUYKQWKWX7). The holiday extensions help, but each store has its own purchase dates, end dates and exclusions. This guide puts the 2026 rules for Walmart, Target, Amazon, Best Buy and Costco side by side, shows how to work out your own last day, and covers gift returns. Every detail comes from the stores' official pages, linked in [Sources](#sources).

**Last checked: Nov 3, 2026.** Every Item is an independent guide and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Walmart, Target, Amazon, Best Buy or Costco. Stores can change these rules at any time, so confirm on the official page before you rely on a date.

## The 2026 holiday return deadlines at a glance

The extra time depends on **when you bought** the item, not when you opened it. Buy outside a store's dates and the normal window applies.

| Store | Bought between | Return by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Walmart | Oct. 1 to Dec. 31 | Jan. 31 (2027) |
| Target (electronics, entertainment, most Target Plus) | Nov. 1 to Dec. 24 | Jan. 24, 2027 |
| Target (Apple, Beats, unlocked or prepaid phones) | Nov. 1 to Dec. 24 | Jan. 8, 2027 |
| Amazon (most items) | Nov. 1 to Dec. 31 | Jan. 31, 2027 |
| Amazon (Apple-branded) | Nov. 1 to Dec. 31 | Jan. 15, 2027 |
| Best Buy (most purchases) | Oct. 30 to Dec. 31 | Jan. 15, 2027 |
| Best Buy (Plus and Total members) | Oct. 30 to Dec. 3 | Jan. 31, 2027 |
| Costco | No holiday window | Normal rules apply |

What each store leaves out of the extension:

- **Walmart:** wireless phones, protection plans, major appliances, Dyson products, and select seasonal and luxury goods.
- **Target:** everything outside the categories above keeps its normal policy. Target Plus seasonal holiday décor is excluded, and the phone extension names only unlocked or prepaid phones.
- **Amazon:** the extension covers "most items". Non-returnable and final sale items stay non-returnable.
- **Best Buy:** activatable devices (phones and similar), AppleCare+ and other third-party contract items, and holiday decorations. Members who buy on or after Dec. 4 get 60 days.
- **Costco:** listed electronics and major appliances must come back within 90 days of when you received them.

## Walmart: Oct. 1 to Dec. 31 purchases, returnable until Jan. 31

[Walmart's return policy](https://corporate.walmart.com/policies) (updated Oct 9, 2026) has an **Extended Holiday Returns** line: most items purchased between October 1 and December 31 are returnable until January 31. The page doesn't print a year. Because it follows the Dec. 31 purchase cutoff, that means Jan. 31, 2027.

Walmart says some exceptions may apply, giving these examples: wireless phones, protection plans, major appliances, Dyson products, and select seasonal and luxury goods. Per the same page, some of them have shorter windows of their own: 14 days for most wireless phones, 30 days for items such as consumer electronics and protection plans, and 2 days for major appliances. For the full list, see our [Walmart return policy guide](/guides/walmart-return-policy/).

Items sold by third-party sellers on Walmart.com have their own section of the policy, and it says most Marketplace items are returnable within 30 days. The holiday line doesn't say whether Marketplace sellers take part, so check the return details on the item page before you count on January.

## Target: a narrow extension for electronics, Apple and phones

Target is the one store here where the holiday rule is easy to misread. Its [extended holiday return policy](https://www.target.com/help/articles/holiday-help/holiday-help-faqs) covers purchases made **Nov. 1 through Dec. 24, 2026**, and the clock starts **Dec. 26, 2026**:

| What you bought | Window from Dec. 26 | Last day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Electronics and entertainment (not Apple or Beats) | 30 days | Jan. 24, 2027 |
| Apple and Beats products, and unlocked or prepaid mobile phones | 14 days | Jan. 8, 2027 |
| Most Target Plus items (not seasonal holiday décor) | 30 days | Jan. 24, 2027 |

Target's [return policy page](https://www.target.com/help/article/000061980) says that for all other items, the normal Target and Target Plus Partner policy applies. For most unopened items sold by Target, that is already **90 days**, so a sweater bought on Nov. 20 isn't getting a January deadline, but it does have until roughly mid-February. Target Owned Brand items can go back within a year with a receipt, and its help pages say holiday and limited-time-only collections may have modified return policies.

Target says Target Circle Card holders and Target Circle 360 members get an extra 30 days on nearly all items bought with their Target Circle Card. Its holiday page points you to the return policy for those details and doesn't say how the extra 30 days combines with the holiday dates, so check your purchase history for the date Target shows. See the [Target return policy guide](/guides/target-return-policy/) for the rest.

## Amazon: Nov. 1 to Dec. 31 purchases, returnable until Jan. 31

The [Amazon Return Policy](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GKM69DUUYKQWKWX7) has a **2026 Holiday Season Extended Return Window**: most items purchased between November 1 and December 31, 2026 can be returned through January 31, 2027. Apple-branded products purchased in the same period can be returned through January 15, 2027.

Two things to keep in mind:

- **It's based on purchase date.** An order placed on Oct. 31 isn't in the extension, so it gets the normal window of 30 days from delivery for most items (15 days for Apple-brand products in new condition).
- **Non-returnable items stay non-returnable.** The same page lists items you can't return, such as perishables, customized products and Amazon Pharmacy items, and says "Final Sale" items can't be returned or refunded.

The fees still apply too: the late fee applies if you start a return but miss the "return by date" shown in your confirmation email or in Your Orders. Our [Amazon return policy guide](/guides/amazon-return-policy/) covers the details, and the [damage fee guide](/guides/amazon-return-damage-fee/) explains deductions for opened items.

## Best Buy: Oct. 30 to Dec. 31 purchases, returnable until Jan. 15

[Best Buy's Return & Exchange Policy](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/help-topics/return-exchange-policy/pcmcat260800050014.c?id=pcmcat260800050014) lists extended holiday return and exchange periods. Purchases made **Oct. 30 through Dec. 31** have an extended return period **through Jan. 15, 2027**, with these exceptions:

| Qualifying purchase | Last day of return window |
| --- | --- |
| Most Best Buy purchases | Jan. 15, 2027 |
| My Best Buy Plus and Total members, purchases made Oct. 30 to Dec. 3, 2026 | Jan. 31, 2027 |
| My Best Buy Plus and Total members, purchases made on or after Dec. 4, 2026 | 60 days |
| Activatable devices (cell phones, cellular tablets, mobile hotspots, cellular wearables) | 14 days (30 days for Verizon activatable devices) |
| Holiday decorations | 15 days |

Best Buy says the holiday extension excludes items bought with a third-party contract, such as activatable devices and AppleCare+ plans, and holiday decorations. Restocking fees, non-returnable products and its other terms still apply, so a camera or drone that qualifies for the January date can still carry a restocking fee if opened. Day 1 of the window is the date you get the product. See our [Best Buy return policy guide](/guides/best-buy-return-policy/).

## Costco: no holiday window, but a 90-day rule on big gifts

[Costco's return policy](https://customerservice.costco.com/app/answers/answer_view/a_id/1191) (published Oct 2, 2025) doesn't mention a holiday return window, so we found no special holiday extension. Costco describes a satisfaction guarantee with a few exceptions. The exception that matters for gifts is that Costco accepts returns within **90 days from the date the member received the merchandise** for:

- Televisions and projectors
- Major appliances (refrigerators above 10 cu. ft., freezers, ranges, cooktops, over-the-range and under-counter microwaves, range hoods, dishwashers, water heaters, washers and dryers)
- Computers, touchscreen tablets and smart wearable devices
- Cameras, aerial cameras (drones), camcorders and MP3 players
- Cellular phones (Costco says return details vary by carrier service contract)

For other products, the policy doesn't list a deadline, though Costco's guarantee carries limitations. Costco also lists items it won't take back, including Shop Cards, gift card and ticket items, airline and live performance event items, and precious metals. Read more in our [Costco return policy guide](/guides/costco-return-policy/).

## How to work out your own return deadline

1. **Find the purchase date, not the unwrapping date.** The holiday windows above are decided by when the item was bought. If it was a gift, ask the giver for the order date, or look at the gift receipt or packing slip.
2. **Check that the date falls inside the store's holiday dates.** Walmart: Oct. 1 to Dec. 31. Best Buy: Oct. 30 to Dec. 31. Amazon: Nov. 1 to Dec. 31. Target: Nov. 1 to Dec. 24, and only for the categories in its table.
3. **Check your item against the exclusions.** Phones, appliances, protection plans, Apple products (at Amazon and Target) and decorations are the usual ones. Excluded items go back to the normal window.
4. **Use the fixed date, or count from the right day.** Walmart, Target, Amazon and Best Buy publish a fixed last day. For everything else, count from the day the store says the clock starts: delivery at Amazon and Costco, the day you receive the product at Best Buy.
5. **Put the last day in your calendar, a few days early.** Aim to return the item well before the deadline.

Some worked examples, using the rules above:

| What you bought | Where and when | Your deadline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A TV | Best Buy, bought Nov. 27 | Jan. 15, 2027 (Jan. 31 if you're a My Best Buy Plus or Total member) |
| A video game console (not Apple) | Target, bought Nov. 20 | Jan. 24, 2027 |
| AirPods | Target, bought Nov. 20 | Jan. 8, 2027 |
| A sweater | Target, bought in store Nov. 20 | 90 days under the normal policy, so Feb. 18, 2027 |
| A TV | Costco, received Nov. 15 | 90 days after receipt, so Feb. 13, 2027 |
| A blender | Amazon, bought Nov. 20 | Jan. 31, 2027 |
| An iPad | Amazon, bought Nov. 20 | Jan. 15, 2027 |
| A jacket | Walmart, bought Oct. 12 | Jan. 31, 2027 |
| A jacket | Amazon, bought Oct. 30 | Not in the holiday window, so 30 days from delivery |

## Gift returns: what each store says

If you're the one holding an unwanted present, the receipt matters as much as the date.

| Store | What the official page says |
| --- | --- |
| Walmart | Items on a gift receipt can be exchanged, refunded to a Walmart gift card, or credited to the sender's original payment. Without a gift receipt, they can be exchanged or refunded to a Walmart gift card |
| Target | In store, a gift receipt or packing slip gets you a Target GiftCard, and it can't be processed the same day the gift was bought. Without either, you may get a merchandise return card. For online gifts, you can return by mail by calling Target.com Guest Services. Target Plus items need a receipt or the original payment method for a lookup |
| Amazon | On the Returns page, use the 17-digit order number from the packing slip or digital gift receipt. If you can't find it, ask the giver. For an exchange, you return the gift and place a new order |
| Best Buy | Store returns of gifts get store credit for the purchase price. Gifts returned by mail are credited to the purchaser, who is notified by email |
| Costco | Costco's pages don't describe gift returns. They say a receipt or the original packaging helps but may not be necessary, so ask at the returns counter |

Practical tips:

- **Ask for a gift receipt when you buy**, or when you give. It proves the purchase date, which decides which window applies.
- **Keep gifts unopened if you're not sure.** Many policies treat opened items differently. Read our guide on [returning opened items](/guides/can-you-return-opened-items/).
- **Mind the early buyers.** A present bought before a store's start date (for example, Amazon on Oct. 30) misses the extension, so tell the recipient to return it early.

## How to never miss a holiday return deadline

- **Photograph every receipt the day you buy.** Gift receipts included.
- **Write the real last day next to the receipt photo**, using the table above, so you don't have to work it out again in January.
- **Keep the box and accessories** until the deadline passes. Missing parts can mean a denied return or a deduction at some stores.
- **Set a reminder a week before**, not on the last day.
- **Don't assume every store counts the same way.** Some publish a fixed last day, and others count from delivery or the day you receive the item.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is the last day to return holiday gifts in 2026?

It depends on the store. Most Walmart items bought Oct. 1 to Dec. 31 go back until Jan. 31, and so do most Amazon items bought Nov. 1 to Dec. 31 (Apple-branded products: Jan. 15). Most Best Buy purchases from Oct. 30 to Dec. 31 go back until Jan. 15, 2027. Target's extended dates are Jan. 24 and Jan. 8, 2027, for specific categories.

### Does Target have extended holiday returns in 2026?

Yes, but only for some items. Purchases made Nov. 1 to Dec. 24, 2026 get a window that starts Dec. 26: electronics and entertainment (not Apple or Beats) and most Target Plus items until Jan. 24, 2027, and Apple, Beats and unlocked or prepaid phones until Jan. 8, 2027. Other items follow the normal policy, which is 90 days for most items sold by Target.

### Does Costco extend returns for the holidays?

Costco's return policy doesn't mention a holiday window. Most products have no listed deadline, while televisions, computers, tablets, cameras, phones, major appliances and a few other electronics must come back within 90 days of when you received them.

### Are phones and appliances covered by holiday return extensions?

Mostly no. Walmart lists wireless phones and major appliances as exceptions. Best Buy excludes activatable devices such as cell phones. At Target, only unlocked or prepaid phones are in the extension, and contract phones aren't. Costco says phone returns vary by carrier contract.

### Does a holiday return window apply if I bought the item before it starts?

No. Each extension is tied to purchase dates, such as Nov. 1 to Dec. 31 at Amazon. An item bought earlier follows the store's normal window, so check the order date.

### Can I return a gift without a receipt?

Often, but you may get store credit instead of a refund, and some stores limit or deny it. Walmart says a return with no receipt gets a Walmart gift card (valid government photo ID required), Target says you may get a merchandise return card, and Best Buy says returns lacking proof of purchase are store credit and may be denied or limited. Costco says a receipt helps but may not be necessary. Keep any gift receipt you're given.
