Open a digital camera from Best Buy and decide you don't want it, and you can lose **15% of the purchase price** to a restocking fee, according to [Best Buy's Return & Exchange Policy](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/help-topics/return-exchange-policy/pcmcat260800050014.c?id=pcmcat260800050014). Opening a box doesn't usually make an item non-returnable, but it can cost you a fee, limit you to an exchange, or rule out a refund for certain categories. This guide covers what Amazon, Target, Walmart, Best Buy and Costco actually say about opened items. Every detail comes from official store pages, linked in [Sources](#sources).

**Last checked: Nov 2, 2026.** Every Item is an independent guide and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Amazon, Target, Walmart, Best Buy or Costco.

## The short answer, store by store

What you'll hear at a returns counter can vary, so it helps to know what the written policy says before you go. Here's the summary for a typical opened item that works fine and has all its parts:

| Store | Opened items in general | Main catch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Amazon](#amazon-original-condition-or-pay-a-fee) | Returnable within the window in original or unused condition | Damage fee of up to 50% if it's not in original condition; 100% restocking fee on opened software, video games, board games and collectible cards or figurines |
| [Target](#target-opened-electronics-calculators-and-earbuds) | No general rule on opened items in its exceptions list | Opened music, movies, video games and software: exchange only. The online return center may decline an opened item |
| [Walmart](#walmart-exchange-only-once-opened) | Most items within 90 days; no blanket "unopened" rule | Some items become exchange-only once opened or used, such as unlocked phones, drones and projectors |
| [Best Buy](#best-buy-opened-cameras-and-restocking-fees) | Returnable in like-new condition with all accessories and packaging | 15% fee on opened cameras, lenses, drones, projectors and more; $45 on opened phones; some items can't come back once opened |
| [Costco](#costco-opened-items-and-the-satisfaction-guarantee) | Covered by the satisfaction guarantee, which doesn't mention opened items | TVs, computers, cameras, phones, major appliances and some other electronics: 90 days |

## Items that are hard to return once opened

These categories are where "it's opened" really matters:

| Item | Amazon | Target | Walmart | Best Buy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Physical music, movies, video games and software | Opened, activated or used software and video games: 100% restocking fee | Exchange for the same title only | Downloadable games and software aren't returnable; physical copies aren't singled out | Exchange for the identical item only (nonreturnable once opened from Marketplace sellers) |
| Board games, collectible cards and figurines | Opened: 100% restocking fee | Trading cards are final sale; opened collectibles are final sale | Trading cards are final sale, opened or not | Trading cards are nonreturnable |
| Phones | Apple products sold new: 15 days | Restocking charge up to $35 if opened; Apple and phones: 14 days | Unlocked phones: exchange only within 14 days once opened | Opened activatable devices: $45 fee |
| Personal-care and hygiene items | Hygiene seals and liners must be intact; items that may pose health and safety risks aren't returnable | Opened breast pumps are final sale; most beauty items are returnable opened within 90 days with a receipt | Hygienic medical equipment, pregnancy and home test kits, and sex toys are final sale | Products that interact with bodily fluids are nonreturnable once opened |

Costco's policy doesn't list any of these categories as exceptions, but it does put a 90-day limit on the electronics listed [below](#costco-opened-items-and-the-satisfaction-guarantee).

## Amazon: original condition, or pay a fee

The [Amazon Return Policy](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GKM69DUUYKQWKWX7) says most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery as long as they're in **original or unused condition**. Amazon asks you to send items back with tags attached, hygiene seals and liners intact, and in the original manufacturer's packaging with all components, accessories, manuals and inserts.

Opening the box isn't the problem on its own. The fees are:

- **Damage fee: up to 50% of the item price** (100% for Amazon Luxury items) if the item is damaged, missing parts, not in original condition, has tags removed or shows obvious signs of use, unless Amazon or the seller caused it.
- **Restocking fee: 100% of the item price** for software and video games that are opened, activated, used or missing parts, and for opened collectible cards, board games and tabletop games, and collectible figurines.

So an opened item that's complete and still in original condition can go back without a fee, but an opened video game effectively gets no refund. For more on how the condition check works, see our [Amazon return damage fee guide](/guides/amazon-return-damage-fee/).

**Holiday purchases:** as of Nov 2, 2026, Amazon's policy says most items bought from Nov 1 through Dec 31, 2026 can be returned through Jan 31, 2027, and Apple-branded products through Jan 15, 2027.

## Target: opened electronics, calculators and earbuds

[Target's returns help page](https://www.target.com/help/articles/returns-exchanges/returns) doesn't have one rule for opened items. Instead it lists specific exceptions:

- **Exchange only once opened:** music, movies, video games and software (for the same title, on the same or a different platform), and airbeds (for a similar item).
- **Final sale:** trading cards, opened or defective collectibles, personalized items, digital downloads, opened or unsealed breast pumps, gift cards and select Target Plus Partner items.
- **Opened beauty:** most beauty items can be returned even if opened, within 90 days with your receipt (120 days with a Target Circle Card).
- **Phones:** a restocking charge of up to $35 may apply, except where prohibited, and there's none if the phone is unopened.

Target also says its **online return center** may decline a return if the item was opened, appears worn or used, or is final sale. And for mail returns, if an item turns out to be ineligible after inspection, Target may charge your card again for a refund it already issued.

**Can you return an opened calculator to Target?** Target doesn't list calculators anywhere in its exceptions, so an opened calculator isn't final sale or exchange-only. The question is the window. Target says electronics and entertainment items (other than Apple and Beats) must come back within **30 days**, and its [returns overview](https://www.target.com/returns) says some items, including electronics, have their own return policies, so check the product page or your receipt. Bring it back complete, with the receipt or the card you paid with, and don't count on a refund for a damaged one.

**Earbuds and headphones:** they aren't on Target's final-sale list. Apple and Beats products, including AirPods and Beats earbuds, have **14 days**; other electronics have 30.

**Video games:** once opened, they can only be exchanged for the same title.

For windows, receipts and refunds, see our [Target return policy guide](/guides/target-return-policy/).

## Walmart: exchange only once opened

[Walmart's Return Policy](https://corporate.walmart.com/policies) (updated Oct 9, 2026) gives **90 days** for most items and **30 days** for consumer electronics and PCs. It doesn't require items in general to be unopened, but some products **can only be exchanged** for the same or a similar item once they're opened or used:

| Item | Once opened or used |
| --- | --- |
| Unlocked wireless phones | Exchange only, within 14 days |
| Drones and projectors | Exchange only, within 30 days |
| Air beds and air mattresses, power generators, durable medical equipment | Exchange only, within 90 days |

Walmart says new, unopened versions of these can still be returned for a full refund. A few other opened-item rules:

- **PCs and PC components** must be returned in their original packaging.
- **Electronics bundles** must be returned together to get a refund.
- **Downloadable video games and software** aren't returnable, and trading cards are final sale whether opened or not.
- **Opened or installed major appliances** past the 2-day window go to the manufacturer warranty.
- **Marketplace sellers** may charge restocking fees of up to 20%, for example on worn, resized or altered watches and jewelry, apparel or collectibles.

Most items bought from Oct 1 through Dec 31 can be returned until Jan 31, with exceptions such as wireless phones, protection plans and major appliances. See our [Walmart return policy guide](/guides/walmart-return-policy/) for the rest.

## Best Buy: opened cameras and restocking fees

[Best Buy's Return & Exchange Policy](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/help-topics/return-exchange-policy/pcmcat260800050014.c?id=pcmcat260800050014) accepts opened items, but they need to be in **like-new condition**. Items that are damaged, unsanitary, dented, scratched or missing major contents may be denied. If accessories or packaging are missing, Best Buy may refuse the return or take a nonrefundable deduction for what's missing.

**Opened cameras cost 15%.** Best Buy charges a restocking fee of **15% of the purchase price** on drones, digital cameras, camera lenses and camcorders (including action cameras), plus projectors and projector screens, golf launch monitors, some scooters and electric wheelchairs, recovery systems, premium cold tubs and saunas, and special-order products. A point-and-shoot is a digital camera, so it's on the list. Opened activatable devices, such as phones (excluding prepaid phones), have a **$45** fee.

There's **no restocking fee** if the product is unopened, or if you bought it in Alabama, Colorado, Hawaii, Iowa, Mississippi, Ohio, Oklahoma or South Carolina, or where the law prohibits it.

**Nonreturnable once opened:** products that interact with bodily fluids, SIM kits, LEGO products, and consumables such as batteries, filters, ink and health test kits. Opened physical software, movies, music, video games and books from Best Buy can only be exchanged for the identical item.

**How long do you have?** For most products, 15 days from the day you receive the item (60 days for paid My Best Buy Plus and Total members). Most purchases made Oct 30 through Dec 31, 2026 can be returned through Jan 15, 2027, and Best Buy says restocking fees still apply during the holiday period. If the camera arrived damaged, defective or wasn't what you ordered, Best Buy says to return it within the window and it will arrange a replacement. Details are in our [Best Buy return policy guide](/guides/best-buy-return-policy/).

## Costco: opened items and the satisfaction guarantee

[Costco's return policy](https://customerservice.costco.com/app/answers/answer_view/a_id/1191) is built on its **Risk-Free 100% Satisfaction Guarantee**: it says it guarantees your satisfaction on every product it sells and will refund the purchase price, with a list of exceptions. The policy **doesn't say items need to be unopened**, and it doesn't list a restocking fee. Costco adds that limitations apply.

The main exception for opened gadgets is timing. TVs, projectors, computers, touchscreen tablets, smart wearables, cameras, drones, camcorders, MP3 players, cell phones and major appliances must be returned **within 90 days** of when you received them. Costco's [warehouse returns page](https://customerservice.costco.com/app/answers/answer_view/a_id/712/~/how-can-i-return-an-item-purchased-in-a-costco-warehouse) says the receipt or original packaging helps, but may not be necessary.

Things Costco won't refund have nothing to do with being opened: custom-made products, airline and live event items, precious metals, Shop Cards, and gift card and ticket items, plus cigarettes and alcohol where the law prohibits returns. See our [Costco return policy guide](/guides/costco-return-policy/).

## How to return an opened item without losing money

- **Check the fee before you open it.** For cameras, phones, games and software, the price of opening the box is written in the policy. Decide whether the fee is worth it first.
- **Keep everything:** box, inserts, cables, manuals and tags. Amazon, Best Buy and Target all treat missing parts or used, damaged items as grounds for a reduced refund or a refusal.
- **Choose the return reason that matches what happened.** Damage and defect rules are different from change-of-mind rules, and stores inspect returns.
- **Wipe your data** from phones, cameras, tablets and laptops before you hand them back. Amazon and Walmart both ask you to erase personal information first.
- **Consider returning in person** for opened items. With mail returns, Target says it may charge your card again if the item fails inspection after you've been refunded.
- **Don't wait.** Electronics windows are short: 14 days for Apple at Target, 15 days at Best Buy, 30 days for electronics at Target and Walmart.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you return an opened calculator to Target?

Target doesn't list calculators as final sale or exchange-only, so an opened one can go back within its return window if it's complete and undamaged. Electronics (other than Apple and Beats) have 30 days, so check the product page or your receipt for the exact window.

### Does Best Buy charge a restocking fee on opened cameras?

Yes. Opened digital cameras, camera lenses, camcorders and drones have a restocking fee of 15% of the purchase price. There's no fee if the camera is unopened, or if you bought it in AL, CO, HI, IA, MS, OH, OK or SC, or where the law prohibits it.

### Can you return opened video games?

At Target and Best Buy, opened physical video games can only be exchanged for the same title. Amazon charges a restocking fee of 100% of the item price on opened, activated or used video games. Walmart doesn't accept returns of downloadable games.

### Does Costco take back opened items?

Costco's satisfaction guarantee covers every product it sells, with listed exceptions, and the policy doesn't say items must be unopened. Opened TVs, computers, tablets, cameras, phones, major appliances and some other electronics must come back within 90 days of when you received them.

### Can you return opened earbuds?

Often, if it's within the window and everything is in the box. Target doesn't list earbuds as final sale (Apple and Beats get 14 days). Amazon requires hygiene seals and liners to be intact, and Best Buy won't take back opened products that interact with bodily fluids, so check before you open them.

### Will I get a full refund for an opened item?

Not always. Amazon can deduct up to 50% for items that aren't in original condition, Best Buy charges restocking fees on cameras, phones and other listed products, and Target may charge up to $35 on an opened phone. Missing accessories can also reduce or block a refund.
