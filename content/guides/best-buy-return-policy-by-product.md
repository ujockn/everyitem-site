Open a point-and-shoot camera from Best Buy, change your mind, and the return costs **15% of what you paid**, according to [Best Buy's Return & Exchange Policy](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/help-topics/return-exchange-policy/pcmcat260800050014.c?id=pcmcat260800050014). Open a TV or a game console and send it back the same way, and Best Buy's fee list has nothing for it. What you're returning decides the fee. This guide goes product by product: TVs, cameras, major appliances, game consoles and toys. It covers the window, the fee, and how the return actually works for each. Every detail comes from Best Buy's official pages, linked in [Sources](#sources). Policies change, so check the official page before you rely on it.

**Last checked: November 12, 2026.** Every Item is an independent guide and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Best Buy.

For the full policy (receipts, refunds, nonreturnable items), see our main [Best Buy return policy guide](/guides/best-buy-return-policy/).

## Best Buy returns by product: the short answer

These are the standard windows for purchases made before Oct. 30, 2026 or after Dec. 31, 2026. For gifts and holiday buys, see [the holiday window](#does-the-holiday-window-change-anything) below.

| Product | Window (standard / paid members) | Fee if opened |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [TVs](#tvs) | 15 / 60 days | None listed |
| [Digital cameras, lenses, camcorders](#cameras-point-and-shoot-mirrorless-action-cameras) | 15 / 60 days | 15% |
| Drones, projectors | 15 / 60 days | 15% |
| [Major appliances](#major-appliances) | 15 / 60 days\* | None listed |
| [Game consoles](#game-consoles-and-video-games) | 15 / 60 days | None listed |
| Opened physical video games | Exchange only | n/a |
| [Toys](#toys-lego-and-trading-cards) | 15 / 60 days | None listed |
| Phones, cellular tablets, hotspots | 14 days (Verizon: 30) | $45 |
| Special-order products | 15 / 60 days | 15% |

\*The current policy page has no separate row for major appliances, so they fall under "most products."

Two shipping limits matter for big items: **TVs over 50 inches and major appliances can't be sent back with Best Buy's prepaid label**. The $45 phone fee doesn't apply to prepaid phones, and opened LEGO and trading cards can't be returned at all.

The window starts **the day you receive the item**, not the day you order it, and it's the same for new, open-box, clearance, refurbished and pre-owned products. The longer member windows don't apply to items sold by Marketplace (third-party) sellers.

## Which Best Buy products have a restocking fee?

Best Buy's fee list is short, and it's per product category, not per price:

| Product | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Activatable devices (cell phones, cellular tablets, mobile hotspots, cellular wearables), excluding prepaid phones | $45 |
| Drones, digital cameras, camera lenses, camcorders (including action cameras), golf launch monitors, premium scooters, super scooters, mobility scooters, electric wheelchairs, leg and body recovery systems, projectors, projector screens, premium cold tubs, premium saunas and special-order products | 15% of the purchase price |

Three rules decide whether you actually pay it:

- **Unopened means no fee.** Best Buy's exception is for unopened products, so a camera you opened but barely used doesn't qualify.
- **Eight states are exempt.** Best Buy charges no restocking fee on purchases made in **Alabama, Colorado, Hawaii, Iowa, Mississippi, Ohio, Oklahoma or South Carolina**, or where the law prohibits it. In some other states, the fee itself is taxed.
- **Membership doesn't waive it.** Best Buy's policy doesn't list membership as an exception, and the [My Best Buy Plus terms](https://storage.bestbuy.com/geeksquad/terms/bestbuy_plus/bby_plus_current_english.pdf) say the rest of the return policy still applies to members. A paid membership gives you more days, not a free return on a camera.

Anything that isn't on the list, including TVs, laptops, game consoles and most toys, has no restocking fee on Best Buy's page. That's not the same as "any condition is fine": every return still has to be [like new](#what-like-new-means-for-big-electronics).

## TVs

**Window:** 15 days, or 60 days for My Best Buy Plus and Total members. Best Buy's policy doesn't list a TV-specific window.

**Fee:** TVs aren't on the restocking fee list. **Projectors and projector screens are**, at 15%, so a home-theater return can cost money if you also bought a projector.

**The gotcha is size, not money.** Best Buy says **TVs over 50 inches can't be returned with its prepaid shipping label**. You can take any Best Buy purchase back to any Best Buy store, but a big TV has to fit in your car. If your TV came on a delivery truck, Best Buy asks you to call **1-888-BEST BUY (1-888-237-8289)** for return and pickup options instead of going to the store.

**Before you return a TV:**

1. Keep the box, the foam inserts, the stand, screws, remote and cables until the window closes. Missing parts can mean a denied return or a deduction from your refund.
2. Sign out of streaming apps and reset the TV. Best Buy asks that connected devices have all data removed and be unlinked from any account.
3. Check the screen and frame for scratches before you pack it. Best Buy can deny returns that are damaged, dented or scratched.

If the TV arrived damaged or doesn't work, Best Buy says to start a return from your order history during the return window, and it can arrange a replacement.

## Cameras: point-and-shoot, mirrorless, action cameras

**Window:** 15 days, or 60 days for Plus and Total members.

**Fee:** 15% of the purchase price if the camera is opened. That covers **digital cameras, camera lenses and camcorders, including action cameras**, plus **drones**.

**How to avoid it:**

- **Decide before you open the box** if you can. Look at the camera in the store or read reviews first, because an unopened camera has no fee.
- **Check your state.** No fee in AL, CO, HI, IA, MS, OH, OK and SC.
- **If it's defective, say so.** Best Buy says it will arrange a replacement for items that arrive damaged or defective during the return window. For special orders, its order help page says the 15% fee applies when items come back opened **and nondefective**.
- **Remove memory cards and wipe the camera.** Delete photos, unpair the camera from your phone app and sign out of any cloud account.
- **Keep everything:** battery, charger, strap, lens caps and the box. Best Buy says items with a rechargeable battery must come back with the original battery.

The holiday extension doesn't help here: Best Buy says restocking fees still apply during the extended holiday window. For how other stores treat opened cameras, see our [electronics return policy guide](/guides/electronics-return-policy/).

## Major appliances

**Window:** Best Buy's current return table doesn't give major appliances a separate row, so they fall under "most products": 15 days, or 60 for Plus and Total members. Older copies of the policy that still circulate online show different tiers and windows, so if you're close to the deadline, confirm with Best Buy by phone.

**Fee:** none on Best Buy's list for refrigerators, washers, dryers, ranges or dishwashers.

**How returns work:**

1. **Inspect it before you accept delivery.** Best Buy says you can refuse a major appliance at delivery if there's any problem. This is the easiest moment to say no.
2. **After you accept it (or if you hauled it home yourself), contact Best Buy right away** about defects, damage or other issues. Best Buy asks you **not to call the store** and to call **1-888-BEST BUY (1-888-237-8289)**, which is also the number for return and pickup options on anything delivered by truck.
3. **Don't try to ship it.** Major appliances, kegerators and SodaStream makers can't go back with the prepaid label.
4. **Some heavy items go to the manufacturer.** Best Buy says it may require you to return certain heavy items directly to the maker.
5. **Marketplace appliances:** contact the seller through your Best Buy account during the return window and schedule a pickup with them.

A dent can matter: Best Buy lists dented and scratched items among those that may be denied, which is another reason to check the appliance while the delivery crew is still there.

## Game consoles and video games

**Window:** 15 days, or 60 days for Plus and Total members.

**Fee:** game consoles aren't on the restocking fee list.

**What trips people up is everything around the console:**

- **Opened physical games are exchange-only.** Opened physical copies of video games bought from Best Buy can only be exchanged for the identical item. From a Marketplace seller, they can't be returned once opened.
- **Digital games and codes can't be returned.** That includes download codes and prepaid cards.
- **Bundles lose their discount.** If you got a discount or a free item for buying several things together, you lose that benefit if you don't return everything.
- **Wipe and unlink the console.** Best Buy asks that connected devices have all data removed and no longer be linked to any account, or it may not accept the return. Sign out of your PlayStation, Xbox or Nintendo account and do a factory reset.

## Toys, LEGO and trading cards

Best Buy's restocking fee list doesn't include toys, so a returned toy has no fee in the policy. But a few toy-adjacent items have stricter rules:

- **LEGO** can't be returned once opened.
- **Trading cards, including Pokémon cards,** can't be returned at all once purchased.
- **Drones** carry the 15% fee when opened, and **premium and super scooters** do too.
- **Electric bikes and electric mopeds** are nonreturnable.

Everything else still needs to come back in like-new condition with all its parts.

## Does the holiday window change anything?

It changes the date, not the fees. Best Buy says purchases made **Oct. 30 through Dec. 31, 2026** can be returned **through Jan. 15, 2027**. Plus and Total members get **until Jan. 31, 2027** for purchases made Oct. 30 through Dec. 3, and 60 days for purchases on or after Dec. 4. Day 1 is the day you get the product.

The extension doesn't apply to activatable devices (14 days, 30 for Verizon), AppleCare+ and other third-party contracts, or holiday decorations (15 days). And Best Buy says **restocking fees and nonreturnable rules still apply**, so an opened camera bought on Black Friday still costs 15% to return in January. See [holiday return deadlines for 2026](/guides/holiday-return-deadlines-2026/) for other stores.

## What "like new" means for big electronics

Best Buy accepts returns in **like-new condition**. Items that are damaged, unsanitary, dented, scratched or missing major contents may be denied. If accessories or packaging are missing, Best Buy may refuse the return or take a nonrefundable deduction from your refund. For any opened item, see our guide on [returning opened items](/guides/can-you-return-opened-items/).

Without proof of purchase, Best Buy may give store credit only, may deny or limit the return, and won't refund sales tax and fees. If you've already missed the deadline, see [what you can still do after a missed return window](/guides/missed-return-window/).

## How to never miss a Best Buy return deadline

- **Photograph the receipt or packing slip** the day the item arrives, since the window starts on delivery.
- **Note the right window:** 15 days for most products, 60 for paid members, 14 for phones.
- **Test cameras and TVs in the first few days,** before you've lost track of the box.
- **Set a reminder a few days early,** especially for a big TV or appliance that needs a pickup scheduled.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Best Buy charge a restocking fee on TVs?

No TV category is on Best Buy's restocking fee list, so an opened TV returned within the window in like-new condition has no listed fee. Projectors and projector screens do carry a 15% fee.

### Does Best Buy charge a restocking fee on a point-and-shoot camera?

Yes, if it's opened. Digital cameras, lenses and camcorders, including action cameras, carry a fee of 15% of the purchase price. There's no fee on an unopened camera, or on purchases in AL, CO, HI, IA, MS, OH, OK or SC.

### How many days do you have to return a TV to Best Buy?

15 days from the day you receive it, or 60 days if you're a paid My Best Buy Plus or Total member. Purchases made Oct. 30 through Dec. 31, 2026 can go back through Jan. 15, 2027.

### Can you ship a TV back to Best Buy?

Only if it's 50 inches or smaller. Best Buy's prepaid return label can't be used for TVs over 50 inches. Take it to a store, or call 1-888-BEST BUY (1-888-237-8289) for pickup options if it came on a delivery truck.

### Can you return a game console to Best Buy?

Yes, within the normal window and with no listed restocking fee, if it's complete, like new, wiped and unlinked from your account. Opened physical games can only be exchanged for the same title, and digital games can't be returned.

### Does Best Buy charge a restocking fee on toys?

Toys aren't on Best Buy's restocking fee list. But opened LEGO sets can't be returned, trading cards can't be returned at all, and drones and premium scooters carry a 15% fee if opened.

### Do My Best Buy members avoid the restocking fee?

Not according to Best Buy's pages. Paid Plus and Total members get a longer window on most products, but the policy doesn't list membership as a fee exception, and the Plus terms say the rest of the return policy still applies.

### How do you return a major appliance to Best Buy?

Inspect it at delivery and refuse it if anything's wrong. After that, call 1-888-BEST BUY (1-888-237-8289) right away rather than contacting the store. Major appliances can't be shipped back, and some heavy items must go back to the manufacturer.
