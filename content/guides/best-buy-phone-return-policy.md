Open the box on a new phone from Best Buy, decide it's not for you, and the return costs **$45**, according to [Best Buy's Return & Exchange Policy](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/help-topics/return-exchange-policy/pcmcat260800050014.c?id=pcmcat260800050014). Wait too long and you may not be able to return it at all. Most Best Buy purchases made this holiday season can go back until Jan. 15, 2027, but phones keep a 14-day window, and a paid membership doesn't stretch it either.

The short answer: you have **14 days** from the day you get the phone (**30 days** for a Verizon device). The same rule covers cellular iPads and other tablets with a cellular connection. If you paid cash and the phone cost more than $800, Best Buy refunds you **by check**, not in cash. Below is how each piece works, taken from Best Buy's own pages, linked in [Sources](#sources).

## Best Buy phone returns: the short answer

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Return window | 14 days from the day you get it (Day 1 is the day you get it) |
| Verizon phones and devices | 30 days |
| My Best Buy Plus or Total member | Still 14 days (30 for Verizon) |
| Bought Oct. 30 to Dec. 31, 2026 | No holiday extension: still 14 days (30 for Verizon) |
| Restocking fee if opened | $45 (not on prepaid phones) |
| No fee if | Unopened, or bought in AL, CO, HI, IA, MS, OH, OK or SC, or where the law prohibits it |
| Paid more than $800 in cash | Refunded by check within 10 business days |
| Carrier service | You cancel it (Best Buy can do it in store if you ask); carrier charges are yours |

## What counts as an "activatable device"?

Best Buy puts phones in their own group, which it calls **activatable devices**: devices that can be activated on a wireless network. Its policy names four kinds:

- **Cell phones**, including iPhones and Android phones
- **Cellular tablets**, such as an iPad with a cellular (Wi-Fi + Cellular) connection
- **Mobile hotspots**
- **Cellular wearables**, such as a smartwatch with its own cellular connection

The definition is about what the device *can* do, not whether you actually signed up for service. So an **unlocked phone** you never put a SIM in still reads as an activatable device under Best Buy's wording. If you're unsure about a specific model, ask at the store before you open it.

**Wi-Fi-only tablets are different.** A Wi-Fi-only iPad or Android tablet isn't on the activatable list and isn't on the restocking fee list either, so it falls under Best Buy's "most products" rule: 15 days, or 60 days for paid Plus and Total members, and no $45 fee. That's a real difference if you're choosing between the two models as a gift.

## How many days do you have to return a phone to Best Buy?

**14 days**, counted from the day you receive the phone. Best Buy's holiday table spells out that the day you get the product is Day 1. That applies whether you picked it up in a store or had it delivered.

**Verizon is the exception.** Best Buy says Verizon devices that can be activated have a **30-day** return period for all customers. Verizon is the only carrier Best Buy's policy page names. The My Best Buy Plus terms say "select carriers" may have a longer return period, so if your phone is on another carrier, check your receipt or ask the store.

Two things that extend most Best Buy returns don't help here:

- **Paid memberships.** My Best Buy Plus and Total members get 60 days on most products, but Best Buy's return table keeps activatable devices at 14 days for members too. The [My Best Buy Plus terms](https://storage.bestbuy.com/geeksquad/terms/bestbuy_plus/bby_plus_current_english.pdf) say the same thing: the 14-day period for activatable devices still applies to members. The free My Best Buy account doesn't change the window either.
- **The 2026 holiday window.** Most purchases made Oct. 30 through Dec. 31, 2026 can go back until Jan. 15, 2027, but Best Buy excludes items bought with a third-party contract, and it names activatable devices as an example. Phones stay at 14 days (30 for Verizon).

Here's what that means in practice for holiday gifts:

| Phone bought and picked up | Last day to return (14 days) | Verizon phone (30 days) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Friday, Nov. 27, 2026 | Thursday, Dec. 10, 2026 | Saturday, Dec. 26, 2026 |
| Tuesday, Dec. 1, 2026 | Monday, Dec. 14, 2026 | Wednesday, Dec. 30, 2026 |
| Thursday, Dec. 10, 2026 | Wednesday, Dec. 23, 2026 | Friday, Jan. 8, 2027 |

A non-Verizon phone bought in early December for Christmas can be **out of its return window before it's unwrapped**. If you're buying a phone as a gift, buy as late as you can, or keep the receipt handy so the recipient can decide quickly. For other stores' holiday rules, see our [holiday return deadlines for 2026](/guides/holiday-return-deadlines-2026/).

## The $45 restocking fee on opened phones

Best Buy's restocking fee list has one flat amount for this whole group: **$45 for activatable devices, excluding prepaid phones**. You don't pay it when:

- **The device is unopened.** Best Buy's exception is for unopened products, so if there's any chance you'll return it, decide before you break the seal.
- **You bought it in Alabama, Colorado, Hawaii, Iowa, Mississippi, Ohio, Oklahoma or South Carolina**, or anywhere the law prohibits the fee.
- **It's a prepaid phone.** Prepaid phones are carved out of the $45 fee. Prepaid phone *cards* are a different item, and Best Buy doesn't take those back at all.

In some states, the $45 fee is itself taxed. A paid membership doesn't waive it, because Best Buy's policy doesn't list membership as an exception.

If the phone **arrived damaged, is defective or isn't what you ordered**, start the return from your order history on BestBuy.com during the return window. For Best Buy purchases brought back to a store, Best Buy says it can arrange a replacement. Its policy doesn't say whether the $45 fee applies to a defective phone, so ask the store before you hand it over.

## Paid cash for a phone? How the refund works

Best Buy usually refunds you the same way you paid, but its policy has a cutoff for cash:

- **Paid more than $800 in cash:** Best Buy refunds you **by check within 10 business days**, not cash at the register.
- **Paid more than $250 by check, or by a debit card without a major credit card logo:** also refunded by check within 10 business days.
- **Paid partly with a Best Buy gift card:** that part comes back on a new Best Buy gift card or e-gift card.
- **Returned by mail:** Best Buy processes it within 7 to 10 business days after it arrives, and your bank can take up to 10 more days to show it.

So a phone that cost more than $800, paid in cash, means waiting for a check. If you want your money back faster, keep that in mind before you pay. Best Buy's policy doesn't say how the $45 fee is collected on a cash refund, so ask the store if it matters to you.

**Without a receipt,** Best Buy says the refund is store credit, may need an email address, may be denied or limited, and doesn't include sales tax. For a cash purchase, the receipt is your proof of purchase, so keep it with the box.

## Returning a phone with a carrier plan or installment agreement

Buying a phone at Best Buy often means signing up with a carrier or adding a line at the same time. The return has two halves: the **phone** goes back to Best Buy, but the **service** belongs to the carrier.

Best Buy says that when you return a phone or device with a carrier contract, **you're responsible for canceling the service contract and for all carrier charges**. It gives two ways to do it:

1. **Return it at a Best Buy store** and tell the associate you want to cancel your service. Best Buy notes that carrier cancellation policies vary.
2. **Call Best Buy Mobile at 1-877-702-2211** (6 a.m. to midnight Central Time) for instructions on returning the device by mail.

If you don't ask Best Buy to cancel the service, Best Buy warns that the carrier may keep billing you for it. If you paid the carrier a deposit, Best Buy says to contact the carrier directly about it.

**If the phone is on a monthly installment plan,** that agreement is with the carrier, not with Best Buy, and Best Buy's pages don't explain how each carrier closes it out. Before you leave the store, ask for confirmation that both the line and the device financing are canceled, then check your next carrier bill. Also keep in mind that an opened **SIM kit** can't be returned once opened, according to Best Buy's list of nonreturnable items.

## Before you hand it back: wipe the phone and turn off Find My

Best Buy says connected devices must have **all data removed** and must **no longer be linked to any cloud account or other device**, or it may not accept the return. For a phone, that means:

- **iPhone or cellular iPad:** back up, unpair any Apple Watch, sign out of your Apple Account, then go to Settings > General > Transfer or Reset > Erase All Content and Settings. Apple says erasing the device turns off Find My and Activation Lock. Steps are in [Apple's guide for selling or trading in an iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109511). Apple also suggests removing the phone from your list of trusted devices.
- **Android phone:** back up, sign out of your Google Account and any other accounts (Best Buy wants the phone unlinked from cloud accounts), then do a factory reset. Google's [factory reset help page](https://support.google.com/android/answer/6088915?hl=en) says to know your Google Account password and charge the phone first.
- **Take out your SIM and any memory card,** and keep the box, charger, cable, SIM tool and paperwork. Best Buy can deny a return or deduct from your refund for missing accessories or packaging, and it wants items back in like-new condition.

## Open-box, refurbished, pre-owned and Marketplace phones

**Used phones follow the same rules.** Best Buy says its return windows apply to new, clearance, open-box, refurbished and pre-owned products alike. A pre-owned or open-box phone gets the same 14 days (30 for Verizon), and the $45 fee section of the policy doesn't make an exception for used phones.

**Marketplace phones** (sold by a third-party seller on BestBuy.com) get Best Buy's standard return policy unless the listing says otherwise, but member extensions never apply to Marketplace items. To return one, Best Buy says to find the order in your account, notify the seller there, print the prepaid label and ship it to the seller during your return period.

**Bought from a vendor inside the store?** If your purchase at a Best Buy store was made directly with a vendor, Best Buy says that vendor's return policy applies instead of Best Buy's.

**Bought an iPhone from Apple, not Best Buy?** Apple has its own 14-day window and doesn't take back Best Buy purchases. See our [Apple return policy guide](/guides/apple-return-policy/).

## How to never miss a Best Buy phone return deadline

Fourteen days is shorter than you think, especially when the phone is a gift or the first week goes to moving your data over.

- **Write down Day 1 the day you get the phone,** whether that's pickup or delivery. Our guide to [when a return window starts](/guides/when-does-return-window-start/) explains how to count.
- **Use the right number:** 14 days, or 30 for Verizon. Don't assume your membership or the holiday extension covers it.
- **Test it in the first week:** battery, camera, signal on your carrier and the features you bought it for.
- **Keep the box, the accessories and the receipt together** until the window closes, especially if you paid cash.
- **Set a reminder two or three days before the last day,** so you have time to wipe the phone and get to a store.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Best Buy's return policy on a smartphone bought with cash?

You get the same 14 days (30 for Verizon) and the same $45 fee if it's opened. The difference is the refund: if you paid more than $800 in cash, Best Buy refunds you by check within 10 business days instead of handing back cash. Keep the receipt: without proof of purchase, Best Buy refunds store credit only.

### How many days do you have to return a phone to Best Buy?

14 days from the day you get it, counting that day as Day 1. Verizon devices get 30 days. Paid members and holiday buyers get the same 14 days.

### What is Best Buy's return window for activatable phones and other devices?

Activatable devices are cell phones, cellular tablets, mobile hotspots and cellular wearables. They get 14 days for every customer, or 30 days if they're Verizon devices, and they're excluded from the 2026 holiday extension.

### What is Best Buy's iPhone return policy?

An iPhone is an activatable device, so it gets 14 days (30 if it's a Verizon iPhone) and a $45 fee if opened, unless you bought it in one of the eight exempt states. Erase it and sign out of your Apple Account so Find My and Activation Lock are off before you return it.

### Can you return a used or pre-owned phone to Best Buy?

Yes, on the same terms. Best Buy's return windows apply to new, open-box, refurbished and pre-owned products, so a used phone gets 14 days (30 for Verizon) and the $45 fee can apply if it's opened.

### What is Best Buy's return policy for tablets and iPads?

It depends on the model. A cellular tablet or iPad is an activatable device: 14 days and a $45 fee if opened. A Wi-Fi-only tablet falls under "most products": 15 days, or 60 for paid Plus and Total members, with no restocking fee on Best Buy's list.

### Do My Best Buy Plus or Total members get 60 days on phones?

No. Best Buy's policy and the My Best Buy Plus terms keep activatable devices at 14 days for members, or 30 for Verizon devices.

### Does the Best Buy holiday return window cover phones?

No. Best Buy's 2026 holiday window (purchases Oct. 30 to Dec. 31, returnable through Jan. 15, 2027) excludes activatable devices, so phones keep their 14 days (30 for Verizon).

### Do I have to cancel my carrier service when I return a phone to Best Buy?

Yes, that's on you. Tell the store associate you want the service canceled when you return the phone, or call Best Buy Mobile at 1-877-702-2211. If you don't, the carrier may keep charging you, so confirm the line and any installment plan are closed on your next bill.
