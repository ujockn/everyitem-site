This guide explains Target's return policy in plain English: how many days you get, which items have shorter windows, what happens without a receipt, and how fast refunds arrive. Every detail comes from Target's official help pages, linked in [Sources](#sources). Policies change, so check the official page before you rely on it.

**Last checked: Oct 22, 2026.** Every Item is an independent guide and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Target.

## How many days do you have to return to Target?

According to [Target's returns help page](https://help.target.com/help/subcategoryarticle?childcat=Returns&parentcat=Policies+%26+Guidelines), most **unopened items sold by Target in new condition** can be returned within **90 days** for a refund or exchange. For Target.com orders, the clock starts when you receive the item: when it's delivered, delivered by a Shipt shopper, or picked up in store.

| Item | Return window | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Most items sold by Target | 90 days | New, unopened condition |
| Target Owned Brand items | 1 year | With a receipt; refund or exchange |
| Electronics and entertainment (not Apple or Beats) | 30 days | |
| Apple and Beats products, including phones | 14 days | |
| Mobile phones bought in store or via Order Pickup | 14 days | Restocking charge may apply (see below) |
| Most beauty items, even opened | 90 days | With receipt; 120 days if bought with a Target Circle Card |
| Most items sold by Target Plus Partners | 30 days | Third-party sellers on Target.com; receipt required in store |
| Online purchases paid with EBT | 90 days | Required by SNAP |
| Baby and wedding registry gifts | Up to 1 year after the event | Most new, unopened items, using the registry return barcode |
| Wish list gifts | 90 days after purchase | Unopened items |

**Target Circle Card and Target Circle 360 get 30 extra days.** Target says Target Circle debit and credit card holders, and Target Circle 360 members, get an extra 30 days to return nearly all items bought with their Target Circle Card. The extra time doesn't apply to Target Owned Brand items, Target Optical purchases, non-returnable items, contract mobile phones or online SNAP purchases paid with EBT.

## Is there a holiday return window?

Target's help page says **holiday and limited-time-only collections may have modified return policies**, and limited-time designer launches typically have a shortened window of two weeks. Target has a help article on extended holiday returns, but it didn't load when we checked on Oct 22, 2026, so we couldn't confirm 2026 holiday dates. Check Target's returns page and your receipt before counting on extra time.

## Exceptions and final sale items

Target lists these as **final sale** (no returns):

- Trading cards and trading card accessories (for example, Pokémon and sports cards and binders)
- Opened or defective collectibles, such as special edition dolls, action figures and die-cast cars
- Personalized items and digital downloads
- Opened or unsealed breast pumps
- Gift cards, including Target GiftCards and prepaid Visa, Mastercard and American Express gift cards, except where the law requires otherwise
- Select items sold by Target Plus Partners

**Exchange only once opened:**

- Music, movies, video games and software can be exchanged for the same title (same or different platform).
- Airbeds can be exchanged for a similar item.

Purchases from Target Optical, Warby Parker, the pharmacy, Starbucks and Target Tech must be returned to those areas. Target stores can't accept international returns.

## Restocking fee on phones

Target says a **restocking charge of up to $35 may apply to phones**, except where prohibited, and it's taxed in some states. **There's no restocking charge if the phone is unopened.** Phones bought with a carrier contract may also trigger early termination fees, and contract phones must be returned in store with a Target Tech rep.

## Returning to Target without a receipt

Target can often find your purchase for you. Return barcodes and receipts are in your Target account for online orders, and for store purchases paid with a card linked to your account or with Wallet in the Target app. The store can also try a **receipt lookup** if you bring the card you paid with.

Without any proof of purchase, Target says you **may** get a **merchandise return card** instead of a refund. Here's what to know about it:

- You need a **government-issued ID** to get one.
- It works only in Target stores, not on Target.com, and can't be turned into cash.
- Its funds don't expire.

Target says items that are opened, damaged or without a receipt **may be denied** a refund or exchange. Without a receipt, returns may be limited. Target Plus items always need proof of purchase.

## How to return something to Target

1. **In store:** bring the item to Guest Services with your receipt, the return barcode from the Target app, or the card you paid with.
2. **Drive Up:** start the return in the Target app and return it without leaving your car.
3. **By mail:** in your Target account, open the order, choose Return and follow the prompts. Target emails a free label (UPS) or a QR code (FedEx). Include the item, packaging and accessories.
4. **Large items:** most can go back to a store with no stocking fees. For a return pickup, call Target.com Guest Services at 1-800-591-3869.

**Gifts:** with a gift receipt or packing slip, you get a Target GiftCard. Without one, you may get a merchandise return card. In-store gift receipt returns can't be done on the same day the gift was bought.

## How refunds work

Refunds usually go back to your original payment method. Target GiftCard purchases come back as a new Target eGiftCard.

| Refund to | Typical timing after Target approves it |
| --- | --- |
| Target Circle Card | Up to 2 days |
| Third-party credit card | Up to 5 days |
| Mail returns to the Online Return Center | About 7 to 10 business days to approve, after the return arrives |

For mail returns, Target may refund you before it gets the item. If the item turns out to be ineligible, Target may charge your card again. With BOGO deals or free gifts, your refund is reduced by the discount or by the prorated value of the free gift.

## How to never miss the Target return window

- **Keep the receipt, or pay with a card linked to your Target account** so the purchase shows up in Wallet.
- **Take a photo of paper receipts**, especially for Target Owned Brand items you might return months later.
- **Note the right window:** 14 days for Apple and phones, 30 for electronics, 90 for most items.
- **Keep things unopened** until you're sure. Opened items may be refused.
- **Set a reminder** a few days before the deadline.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many days do you have to return something to Target?

Most new, unopened items sold by Target: 90 days. Target Owned Brand items: one year with a receipt. Electronics: 30 days. Apple, Beats and mobile phones: 14 days.

### Can I return to Target without a receipt?

Often, yes. Target can look up purchases made with your card or linked to your Target account. With no proof of purchase at all, you may get a merchandise return card (government ID required), and the return may be limited or denied.

### Does Target Circle give you more time to return?

Target Circle Card holders and Target Circle 360 members get an extra 30 days on nearly all items bought with their Target Circle Card. The extra time doesn't apply to Target Owned Brand items, Target Optical, contract phones or non-returnable items.

### Can I return opened items to Target?

Opened items may be denied a refund. Some exceptions are friendlier: most beauty products can be returned opened within 90 days with a receipt. Opened movies, music, games and software can only be exchanged for the same title.

### Does Target charge a restocking fee?

For phones, a restocking charge of up to $35 may apply, except where prohibited. There's no charge if the phone is unopened.

### How long does a Target refund take?

Once Target approves the refund, it typically takes up to 2 days on a Target Circle Card and up to 5 days on a third-party credit card. Mail returns take an average of 7 to 10 business days to approve after they arrive.
