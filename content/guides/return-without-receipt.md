Lose the receipt for something you bought at Best Buy, with no other proof of purchase, and Best Buy says your refund comes as **store credit**, may be **denied or limited**, and **won't include sales tax and fees**, per its [Return & Exchange Policy](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/help-topics/return-exchange-policy/pcmcat260800050014.c?id=pcmcat260800050014). The good news: a missing paper slip is often not the same as missing proof. Most big stores can find your purchase from the card you paid with, your store account or an order number, and then the return goes through like any other. This guide covers how Walmart, Target, Best Buy, The Home Depot, Lowe's, Amazon and Costco look up a lost receipt, what ID they ask for, and what you get back when nothing can be found. Every detail comes from official store pages, linked in [Sources](#sources).

**Last checked: Nov 19, 2026.** Every Item is an independent guide and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Walmart, Target, Best Buy, The Home Depot, Lowe's, Amazon or Costco. Stores can change these rules at any time, so confirm on the official page before you go.

Returning a gift someone else bought? The rules for gift receipts and gift returns are different. See [returning a gift without a receipt](/guides/return-gift-without-receipt/).

## Store by store: lost receipt vs no proof at all

| Store | How it can find your purchase | If nothing can be found |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Walmart](#walmart) | Debit or credit card for store purchases; phone number or email for online orders | Government photo ID; Walmart gift card if refund verification approves. Number of no-receipt returns is limited |
| [Target](#target) | Your Target account or app Wallet; card you paid with (Target Circle Card, Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover) | Merchandise return card, government ID required. Returns may be limited |
| [Best Buy](#best-buy) | Credit card you paid with; phone, email or member ID for My Best Buy members; online order lookup | Store credit, may need an email address, may be denied or limited, no sales tax or fees back |
| [The Home Depot](#the-home-depot) | Card or mobile wallet you paid with, Pro Xtra number, order number, Purchase History barcode | Driver's license or government photo ID and approval by a refund verification system |
| [Lowe's](#lowes) | Electronic receipt, order number, card or digital wallet, phone number, MyLowe's Rewards account | No return. Lowe's doesn't accept returns without valid proof of purchase |
| [Amazon](#amazon) | Every order is in Your Orders, so no receipt is needed | Not applicable for your own orders |
| [Costco](#costco) | Bring the item to the warehouse | Costco says a receipt helps but may not be necessary |

The pattern: **pay by card or use a store account, and a lost receipt rarely matters.** Pay cash with no account, and you may be in the "nothing can be found" column, usually with store credit instead of money back.

## First, try to find the receipt yourself

Before you drive to the store, spend five minutes looking. Each of these counts as proof at one or more of the stores below:

1. **Search your email** for the order confirmation, shipping confirmation or e-receipt. Target and Best Buy both point to order emails and order pages for online purchases.
2. **Open the store's app or website** and check your order or purchase history. Target keeps return barcodes in your account, and Home Depot's Purchase History shows a barcode you can scan at the desk.
3. **Find the card or phone you paid with.** If you used Apple Pay or another mobile wallet at Home Depot, bring that same device.
4. **Check your card statement** for the date and store, so the service desk knows which day to search.

Found nothing? Bring the item, all its parts and packaging, the card you paid with and a government photo ID. Then ask for a receipt lookup before you ask for a no-receipt return.

## Walmart

Walmart's [return policy](https://corporate.walmart.com/policies) (updated Nov. 2, 2026) says that if you don't have a receipt, it can try to look up **store purchases with the debit or credit card** you used, and **online orders with your phone number or email**. The policy also links to an online store receipt search.

If there's still no receipt, Walmart requires a **valid government-issued photo ID**. The return goes through Walmart's refund verification process, and if it's accepted you get a **Walmart gift card**, not cash or a card refund. Walmart says it limits how many non-receipted returns a customer can make and how often, and that all non-receipt returns are subject to verification.

One state detail: in Connecticut, Massachusetts and Rhode Island, Walmart says you need a receipt with a valid transaction number to get sales tax refunded. More in our [Walmart return policy guide](/guides/walmart-return-policy/).

## Target

Target's help article on [returning without a receipt](https://www.target.com/help/article/000062164) says receipts and return barcodes are saved in your **Target account** for online orders, and for store purchases if you paid with a card linked to your account or with **Wallet in the Target app**. For online orders, the shipping and delivery emails also work.

At Guest Services, Target can try a **receipt lookup** for store and online purchases if you bring the item and the payment you used, within the item's return window. Target lists the cards it can search: Target Circle Cards and third-party Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover credit cards.

Without any proof (receipt, digital barcode or packing slip), you may get a **merchandise return card**. Target's [merchandise return card page](https://www.target.com/help/article/000062592) says you need a **government-issued ID** to get one, it only works in Target stores, and it can't be turned into cash or credit except where law requires. Target's [returns help](https://help.target.com/help/subcategoryarticle?childcat=Returns&parentcat=Policies+%26+Guidelines) adds that returns without a receipt may be limited, and that Target Plus Partner items always need proof of purchase. See our [Target return policy guide](/guides/target-return-policy/) for windows by category.

## Best Buy

Best Buy's [receipt help page](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/help-topics/getting-receipt-copies/pcmcat204400050026.c?id=pcmcat204400050026) says the customer service counter can look up an in-store receipt with the **credit card you paid with**. If you're a **My Best Buy member**, it can also search by phone number, email address or member ID. Members with a linked account can print receipts online, and guest online orders can be found with the order number, last name and phone number.

Best Buy says it **can't retrieve receipts for cash or check purchases** unless you're a My Best Buy member, or for purchases more than ten years old.

If you can't prove the purchase at all, the [Return & Exchange Policy](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/help-topics/return-exchange-policy/pcmcat260800050014.c?id=pcmcat260800050014) says the refund may require an email address, will be **store credit**, may be denied or limited, and **state sales taxes and fees won't be reimbursed**. Best Buy's standard window is only 15 days for most products (60 for paid members), so don't let a receipt hunt run past it. Details in our [Best Buy return policy guide](/guides/best-buy-return-policy/).

## The Home Depot

The Home Depot's [receipt lookup page](https://www.homedepot.com/c/receipt-lookup) says you don't necessarily need a paper receipt to return something in a store. It lists other ways to find the sale:

- the **debit or credit card** you paid with
- the **same phone or device** if you paid with a mobile wallet such as Apple Pay, Samsung Pay or Google Pay
- your **Pro Xtra** number, if you entered it at checkout
- an **order number** for online orders or in-store service agreements
- the barcode on a purchase in your **homedepot.com Purchase History**

The same page says card purchases can be retrieved for up to 24 months, but **cash purchases can't be retrieved** without the original receipt.

For returns with no receipt, Home Depot's [return policy](https://www.homedepot.com/c/Return_Policy) requires a **valid driver's license or government-issued photo ID**, and the return has to pass a third-party refund verification system. The official pages we could read don't spell out how a no-receipt refund is paid, so ask at the service desk before you hand the item over.

## Lowe's

Lowe's is the strict one. Its [Returns, Refunds & Exchanges page](https://www.lowes.com/l/help/returns-policy) says it **does not accept returns or exchanges without a valid proof of purchase**. Proof doesn't have to be paper, though. Lowe's says it can try to validate a purchase with:

- a physical or electronic receipt
- the order number
- the payment card or digital wallet you used
- your phone number
- your MyLowe's Rewards or MyLowe's Pro Rewards account

Lowe's also uses refund verification systems that may ask for a valid photo ID, such as a state driver's license, state ID card or military ID. If you paid cash and gave no phone number or account, there may be nothing to look up. More in our [Lowe's return policy guide](/guides/lowes-return-policy/).

## Amazon

You don't need a receipt for your own Amazon order. Amazon's [return policy](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GKM69DUUYKQWKWX7) says you start a return from **Your Orders** by choosing Return Items, and the "return by date" is shown in your return confirmation and Order History. The receipt problem at Amazon is really a deadline problem, so see our [Amazon return policy guide](/guides/amazon-return-policy/) and what to do if you've [missed a return window](/guides/missed-return-window/).

## Costco

Costco's help page on [warehouse returns](https://customerservice.costco.com/app/answers/answer_view/a_id/712/~/how-can-i-return-an-item-purchased-in-a-costco-warehouse) says it helps to have the receipt or original packaging, **but it may not be necessary**. Bring the item to any Costco warehouse and Member Services will help, with limitations. Costco adds that some items do need to be returned within 90 days. See our [Costco return policy guide](/guides/costco-return-policy/).

## What you usually lose without a receipt

Even when a store accepts a no-receipt return, you often get less:

- **Store credit instead of money back.** Walmart issues a gift card, Target a merchandise return card and Best Buy store credit.
- **Sales tax.** Best Buy says it won't refund sales tax and fees on a return without proof, and Walmart requires a receipt for the tax refund in three states.
- **Future returns.** Walmart and Target say they may limit no-receipt returns, and several stores record your ID for verification.

That's the case for keeping proof of anything you might return, and anything with a warranty. Our guide on [how long to keep receipts](/guides/how-long-to-keep-receipts/) covers what to save and for how long. For bigger purchases, the same receipt helps prove ownership to an insurer, which our [home inventory for insurance guide](/guides/home-inventory-for-insurance/) explains.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you return something without a receipt?

Often, yes. Walmart, Target, Best Buy, Home Depot and Lowe's can look up a purchase from the card you paid with, your account or your order number. If nothing can be found, Walmart, Target and Best Buy may still take it back for store credit, but Lowe's says it won't accept a return without proof of purchase.

### Can a store look up my receipt with my credit card?

At Walmart, Target, Best Buy, Home Depot and Lowe's, yes. Bring the same card you paid with. At Home Depot, if you paid with Apple Pay or another mobile wallet, bring the same phone or device.

### What do you get back if you return something without a receipt?

Usually store credit. Walmart issues a Walmart gift card, Target a merchandise return card that only works in its stores, and Best Buy store credit without sales tax and fees. If the store finds your purchase through a lookup, it's treated as a normal return.

### Do you need ID to return something without a receipt?

At most big stores, yes. Walmart and Home Depot require a government-issued photo ID for non-receipted returns, Target needs one to issue a merchandise return card, and Lowe's may ask for one during refund verification.

### Is there a limit on returns without a receipt?

Walmart says it limits the number and frequency of non-receipted returns, and Target says no-receipt returns may be limited. Best Buy says they may be denied or limited. None of these pages publish an exact number.

### Can you return something you paid for in cash without a receipt?

It's harder. Home Depot says cash receipts can't be retrieved, and Best Buy can't find cash purchases unless you're a My Best Buy member. Without a lookup, you're relying on the store's no-receipt rules, which usually mean ID and store credit, and Lowe's won't take it at all.

### Do you need a receipt to return to Amazon?

No. Your own orders are in Your Orders, and you start the return there. For a gift from someone else, see our guide to returning a gift without a receipt.
