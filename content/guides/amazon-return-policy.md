This guide explains Amazon's return policy in plain English: how long you have, which items get shorter or longer windows, when Amazon charges a fee, and how fast refunds arrive. Every detail comes from Amazon's official help pages, linked in [Sources](#sources). Policies change, so check the official page before you rely on it.

**Last checked: Oct 22, 2026.** Every Item is an independent guide and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Amazon.

## How many days do you have to return to Amazon?

According to the [Amazon Return Policy](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GKM69DUUYKQWKWX7), most items can be returned for a refund or replacement **within 30 days of delivery**, as long as they're in original or unused condition. Your exact deadline is the "return by date" in your return confirmation email or in Your Orders.

| Return window | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| 7 days | Kindle books bought by accident and not read; digital textbooks and educational content not downloaded; songs or albums bought by accident with Alexa |
| 15 days | Apple and Boost Infinite products sold in new condition; Amazon Haul items over $3 |
| 30 days | Most items |
| 90 days | Select Amazon Renewed items in Acceptable, Good or Excellent condition; most nonperishable baby products; mattresses (not crib mattresses); gifts bought from a Birthday or Custom Gift List by someone other than the list owner |
| 180 days | Gifts bought from an Amazon Wedding Registry by someone other than the registry owner |
| 365 days | Amazon Renewed Premium items; gifts bought from an Amazon Baby Registry by someone other than the registry owner |

Check the product page before you buy. Amazon says each item's return eligibility is shown there, and in your order history after you order.

## Is there a holiday return window?

As of Oct 22, 2026, Amazon's official return policy page **doesn't list a 2026 holiday extension**. Amazon has extended its return window for holiday purchases in past years, so it may announce one later. Don't count on extra time until it appears on Amazon's own pages or in your order's "return by date."

## Return fees

Amazon says returns are generally free, and most items include at least one free return option. Fees can apply in these cases:

| Fee | When it applies | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Return shipping fee | You pick a drop-off or shipping option that isn't free, return a heavy or bulky item, or have an unusually high return rate | Varies by item and shipping method |
| Late fee | You don't drop off the item (or complete a carrier pickup) by the return by date | 20% of the item price for the first 30 days after the return by date; 100% after that |
| Damage fee | The item comes back damaged, missing parts, not in original condition, with tags removed or with obvious use (not Amazon's or the seller's fault) | Up to 50% of the item price (100% for Amazon Luxury items) |
| Restocking fee | Software and video games that are opened, activated, used or missing parts; opened collectible cards, board games and collectible figurines | 100% of the item price |

**Heavy or bulky items** are those that weigh 50 lbs or more, have a longest side over 59 inches when packed, or have a girth over 130 inches. These may cost money to return, and some need a home pickup appointment.

## Items you can't return

Amazon lists these as non-returnable:

- Perishables
- Products that may pose health and safety risks if returned
- Products with shipping restrictions
- Customized products made for you
- Redeemable products
- Amazon Pharmacy products and pet medications
- Certain digital products
- Automobiles

Items marked **Final Sale** can't be returned or refunded either. That includes trading card games, some discounted items and Amazon Haul items that cost $3 or less. If a non-returnable item arrives damaged, defective or different from what you ordered, contact Amazon Customer Service.

## Opened items and condition

Return items in **original or unused condition**, with tags attached, hygiene seals and liners intact, and in the original manufacturer's packaging with all components, accessories and manuals. Otherwise you may get a damage fee (see above). If you saved personal information on a computer or other device, erase it by following the manufacturer's instructions before you send it back.

Two more rules worth knowing:

- **Bundles:** you must return every item in a "Bundle with Savings" to get a refund. Partial refunds aren't available.
- **Discounts:** if an item helped you qualify for a discount, Amazon reduces your refund to account for it.

## Receipts, gifts and ID

Amazon orders live in your account, so you don't need a paper receipt. Start a return from **Your Orders** by choosing "Return Items."

**Returning a gift?** Use the 17-digit order number on the packing slip or digital gift receipt on Amazon's Returns page. If you can't find it, ask the gift giver. For a different size or item, return the gift and place a new order.

Amazon says it may ask for more information during a return, such as a **government-issued photo ID**, and it can deny returns or add fees if it suspects fraud or abuse.

## How refunds work

Amazon issues the refund once it (or the third-party seller) receives the item and confirms you're eligible. **It can take up to 30 days to receive and process a return.** After the refund is issued:

| Refund method | Typical timing after the refund is issued |
| --- | --- |
| Amazon gift card balance | 2 to 3 hours |
| Credit card | 3 to 5 business days |
| Shop with Points | Up to 5 business days |
| Debit card, checking account, EBT card, cash (participating locations) | Up to 10 business days |
| Prepaid credit card | Up to 30 days, depending on the issuer |
| Promotional certificate | No refund issued |

**Third-party sellers** that ship their own items handle their own returns. Amazon says they must give you a US return address, a prepaid return label, or a full refund without asking for the item back.

## How to never miss the Amazon return window

- **Look at the return by date** the day you start a return, and drop the item off before it. Late fees start at 20%.
- **Count from the delivery date** and use the right window: 15 days for new Apple products, not 30.
- **Keep the box and every accessory** until you're sure you're keeping the item.
- **Take a photo of the item and packing slip** before you send it back, in case of a dispute.
- **Set a reminder** a few days before the deadline so you have time to get to a drop-off location.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many days do you have to return something to Amazon?

Most items: 30 days from delivery. Some items get less (15 days for new Apple products) and some get more (90 days for most nonperishable baby products and mattresses).

### Are Amazon returns free?

Most items include at least one free return option. You may pay if you choose a paid shipping option, return a heavy or bulky item, return late, or return an item that's damaged or used.

### What happens if I return an Amazon item late?

Amazon may charge a late fee of 20% of the item price for the first 30 days after the return by date, and 100% of the item price after that.

### How long does an Amazon refund take?

Amazon says it can take up to 30 days to receive and process a return. Once the refund is issued, it takes 2 to 3 hours for gift card balance, 3 to 5 business days for credit cards and up to 10 business days for debit cards.

### Can I return an opened video game to Amazon?

Amazon charges a restocking fee of 100% of the item price for software and video games that are opened, activated, used or missing parts, so in practice you won't get money back.

### Does Amazon extend returns for the holidays in 2026?

As of Oct 22, 2026, Amazon's official return policy page doesn't show a 2026 holiday extension. Check the page and your order's return by date later in the season.
