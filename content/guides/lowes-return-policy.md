A new washer that turns out to be the wrong fit has to be returned to Lowe's within **48 hours** of delivery or pickup, not 90 days, according to Lowe's [Returns, Refunds & Exchanges page](https://www.lowes.com/l/help/returns-policy). Wait too long on a custom special order and it costs you too: those returned or canceled more than 72 hours after purchase carry a **20% restocking fee**. For most other things you buy at Lowe's, you have **90 days** with proof of purchase. This guide covers every return window Lowe's lists, what to do without a receipt, gifts and holiday items, how refunds are paid, what can't go back, and where price adjustments fit in. Every detail comes from Lowe's official pages, linked in [Sources](#sources).

## How many days do you have to return to Lowe's?

The standard rule on Lowe's [returns page](https://www.lowes.com/l/help/returns-policy) is **90 days from the original purchase date** for most new, unused products, for a refund or an exchange, as long as you have proof of purchase. You can take the return to **any Lowe's store in the US**.

Two things trim that 90 days. First, a long list of categories has shorter windows, covered below. Second, items sold by **third-party sellers** on Lowes.com (the listing says "Sold and Shipped by" another company) usually get **30 days**. Lowe's also says every return is subject to "system approvals," so a return inside the window can still be declined.

## The 48-hour rule: major appliances and other big items

This is the rule that catches people. Lowe's says the return has to be **started within 48 hours of delivery, or of the time you picked the item up** at a store. It covers:

- **Major appliances:** refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, ranges, range hoods, dishwashers, over-the-range microwaves, cooktops, wall ovens, and washer and dryer pedestals
- **Air conditioners and evaporative coolers** (portable and window units)
- **Paint sprayers** and **tile saws**
- **Construction heat and parts**
- **Utility vehicles, golf carts, go-karts and motorized bikes**, which Lowe's says must come back to a store "in the original state of purchase," with the original paperwork and title if there is one
- **Gas-powered portable generators, pressure washers, chainsaws and pole saws**

There are two ways to get **30 days** instead:

- **Leave it sealed.** An item returned in its original, unopened, undamaged, factory-sealed packaging (or unused, if it wasn't sold in a package) can go back within 30 days of delivery or pickup.
- **Pay with a Lowe's account or card.** Purchases on a Lowe's Commercial Account, MyLowe's Pro Rewards Credit, MyLowe's Pro Rewards American Express, MyLowe's Rewards Credit Card, Lowe's Business Rewards, Lowe's Preload Plus Mastercard or Lowe's Lease-to-Own also get 30 days.

Neither extension applies to **gas-powered portable generators, pressure washers, chainsaws or pole saws**. Lowe's lists those as returnable but not eligible for the 30-day extension.

One wording note: the summary at the top of Lowe's page says "48 hours of purchase," while the detailed section says 48 hours from delivery or pickup. For a delivered fridge, those can be different days. If you're close, call the store the same day rather than guessing.

What this means in practice: unbox and test a new appliance the day it arrives, and check the fit, doors, water line and noise right away. Our guide on [returning opened items](/guides/can-you-return-opened-items/) explains why "opened" changes the answer at many stores.

## 30-day items: mowers, paint, TVs and many plants

Lowe's lists these with a **30-day** window:

- **TVs and electronics**
- **Water heaters** and **HVAC systems**
- **Liquid paint**
- **Special order custom blinds and window treatments** made to your measurements
- **Most outdoor power equipment**, including lawn mowers, leaf blowers, trimmers and trimmer attachments, edgers, tillers, cultivators, augers, chippers, shredders, log splitters, snow blowers, and subcompact tractors and their attachments
- **Annuals, house and patio plants, and Bonnie Plants**

The exception inside the exception: **CRAFTSMAN battery and electric outdoor power equipment** keeps the full **90 days**. A gas mower gets 30 days; a CRAFTSMAN cordless mower gets 90.

## Trees, shrubs, perennials and Lowe's card purchases: 365 days

**Trees, shrubs and perennials** can be returned within **365 days** of purchase. Lowe's [store services page](https://www.lowes.com/l/about/store-services) puts it as a promise: if one doesn't survive a year, bring in the plant and your receipt and Lowe's replaces it free. A paper receipt from March is easy to lose by autumn, so this is the one to photograph.

Lowe's also gives **365 days** to purchases made with a Lowe's Commercial Account, MyLowe's Pro Rewards Credit Card, MyLowe's Rewards Credit Card or Lowe's Business Rewards, unless a shorter rule above applies. The list of cards differs slightly between sections of Lowe's page, so check that your card is named. **Lowe's Pay** purchases don't get the credit card extensions.

## Returning to Lowe's without a receipt

Lowe's is clear: it **does not accept returns or exchanges without a valid proof of purchase**. But proof of purchase doesn't have to mean the paper slip. Lowe's says it can try to find your purchase with:

- a physical or electronic receipt
- the order number
- the payment card or digital wallet you paid with
- your phone number
- your **MyLowe's Rewards** or **MyLowe's Pro Rewards** account

So if you paid by card or gave your phone number or MyLowe's account at checkout, bring that to the customer service desk. For Lowes.com orders, Lowe's [help page](https://www.lowes.com/l/help) says the **return barcode** on your Order Details page works too.

Lowe's uses refund verification systems that may ask for a **valid photo ID**: a state driver's license, state ID card or military ID. If you paid cash and gave no phone number or account, you may have nothing Lowe's can look up. That's the case where saving the receipt matters most. ## Gifts and holiday items

**Gifts:** Lowe's says a gift can be returned **with the original receipt** as long as it's inside the item's return window. Its answer mentions only the original receipt, not a separate gift receipt process, so if you're giving a Lowe's gift, include the receipt or be ready to return it yourself. For gifts from other stores, see [returning a gift without a receipt](/guides/return-gift-without-receipt/).

**Holiday items:** Lowe's says holiday items must be returned **on or before the day of the holiday**, and that anything bought after the holiday is **final sale**. Lowe's stores are closed on Easter, Thanksgiving and Christmas, so a Christmas tree or decoration has to go back by **Dec. 24** in practice. Lowe's returns page doesn't list an extended holiday return window for 2026, so gifts bought in November follow the normal windows above.

That's very different from stores that stretch their windows into January. Our [holiday return deadlines for 2026](/guides/holiday-return-deadlines-2026/) puts the other big stores side by side.

## How Lowe's refunds you

Returns with valid proof of purchase are refunded to the **original payment method**, with these exceptions from Lowe's returns page:

- **Gift card, refund card, merchandise card or in-store credit:** refunded as in-store credit. The same goes for purchases through a Lowe's storefront on a third-party online marketplace.
- **Check:** returns within 3 days of purchase get in-store credit. After 3 days, you may get cash or in-store credit, as Lowe's refund verification decides.
- **Cash or check, delivered or returned by phone or online:** refunded as a **refund voucher** emailed to the address on the order within 24 hours of processing. You can cash it at any Lowe's with a photo ID that matches the name on it.
- **Cash or check over $1,000:** Lowe's may mail a check instead.
- **Lowe's Pay:** credited back to the financing company, which handles the refund with you.
- **MyLowe's Money:** returned to your MyLowe's digital wallet.

Lowe's says it will provide a written copy of its return policy if you ask.

## What Lowe's won't take back

Lowe's lists these as **nonrefundable**:

- Labor and installation charges
- Delivery and shipping charges, unless Lowe's made the mistake
- Gift cards of any kind (Lowe's, Visa, Mastercard or other brands), in-store credits, refund cards and merchandise cards (see [can you return a gift card](/guides/return-gift-card/))
- Safety masks and face coverings
- Highway trailers and whole house generators
- As-is merchandise, paint samples and trading cards
- Used items that may have come into contact with bodily fluids or waste
- **Special order customized** flooring, kitchen cabinets and countertops, interior and exterior doors, patio and storm doors, and windows

**Special order configured products**, such as vanities, garage doors, sheds, shower doors, shutters and window treatments made to your measurements, can come back, but returns or cancellations **more than 72 hours after purchase** carry a **20% restocking fee**, and they have to go back to the store where you bought them.

**Lowe's Outlet** sales are final except for permitted returns. Outlet items may have cosmetic damage or be previous returns, and anything eligible for return must go back to the same Outlet, not a regular store. Inspect Outlet items before you pay.

## How to return a Lowes.com order

1. Open **Order History** on Lowes.com and choose the order. Lowe's help page says you can start a return there.
2. Either follow the steps to send it back, or take it to a store.
3. In a store, go to the **customer service desk** with your proof of purchase. The **return barcode** from the Order Details page counts.
4. If a third-party seller shipped it, check the listing's return terms; most marketplace items get 30 days.

Lowe's help page doesn't spell out shipping labels or refund posting times, so check the instructions inside your order before you pack anything.

If the price drops after you buy, Lowe's returns page says to **chat with Lowe's or call 1-877-465-6937** and it will check whether the identical item qualifies. ## Frequently asked questions

### How many days do you have to return something to Lowe's?

Most new, unused items get 90 days from purchase with proof of purchase. Major appliances and a few other big items get 48 hours from delivery or pickup, many categories get 30 days, and trees, shrubs and perennials get 365 days.

### What is Lowe's return policy on appliances?

Refrigerators, washers, dryers, ranges, dishwashers and other major appliances must be returned within 48 hours of delivery or pickup. If the appliance is still in its unopened, factory-sealed box, or you paid with an eligible Lowe's account or card, you get 30 days.

### Can you return something to Lowe's without a receipt?

Not without some proof of purchase. Lowe's can look up the sale with your order number, the card or digital wallet you paid with, your phone number or your MyLowe's account, and it may ask for a photo ID.

### Does Lowe's do price adjustments?

Lowe's says to chat with it or call 1-877-465-6937 if you find a lower price on an identical item after buying, and it will review the request. It doesn't publish a time limit. See our price adjustment policies guide for the full rules.

### How long do you have to return plants to Lowe's?

Trees, shrubs and perennials get 365 days, and Lowe's says it will replace one that doesn't survive a year if you bring the plant and receipt. Annuals, house and patio plants get 30 days.

### Can you return Christmas decorations to Lowe's after Christmas?

No. Lowe's says holiday items must be returned on or before the holiday, and its stores are closed on Christmas Day, so bring them back by Dec. 24. Holiday items bought after the holiday are final sale.

### Does Lowe's charge a restocking fee?

On special order configured products, such as vanities, garage doors, sheds and custom window treatments, Lowe's charges 20% if you return or cancel more than 72 hours after purchase. Lowe's doesn't list a restocking fee for regular items.

### How do I return a Lowes.com order?

Start the return in Order History on Lowes.com, or take the item to any Lowe's store's customer service desk with your proof of purchase. The return barcode on the Order Details page works as proof.
