This guide explains Best Buy's return policy in plain English: how many days you get, what a membership changes, when you pay a restocking fee, and what you can't return. Every detail comes from Best Buy's official pages, linked in [Sources](#sources). Policies change, so check the official page before you rely on it.

**Last checked: Oct 30, 2026.** Every Item is an independent guide and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Best Buy.

## How many days do you have to return to Best Buy?

According to [Best Buy's Return & Exchange Policy](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/help-topics/return-exchange-policy/pcmcat260800050014.c?id=pcmcat260800050014), the return period **begins the day you receive your product**, not the day you ordered it. The same windows apply to new, clearance, open-box, refurbished and pre-owned products. Best Buy says these standard windows apply to purchases made before Oct. 30, 2026, and after Dec. 31, 2026. For purchases in between, see the [holiday return window](#is-there-a-holiday-return-window) below.

| Product | Standard (free My Best Buy or no account) | My Best Buy Plus and Total members |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Most products | 15 days | 60 days |
| Activatable devices (cell phones, cellular tablets, mobile hotspots, cellular wearables) | 14 days | 14 days |
| Verizon activatable devices | 30 days | 30 days |
| Halloween and Christmas decorations | 15 days | 15 days |
| Litter boxes | 90 days | 90 days |
| Hearing aids and pet training devices | 60 days | 60 days |
| Hearing amplifiers | 15 days | 15 days (Best Buy says they follow the standard 15-day policy) |
| Microsoft Office | 30 days | 30 days |
| Marketplace items (sold by third-party sellers) | 15 days | 15 days (member extension doesn't apply) |

A few things to notice:

- **The 60-day window is only for paid members.** Best Buy's membership page lists three levels: a free My Best Buy account, My Best Buy Plus and My Best Buy Total. The 60-day return window is a Plus and Total benefit. A free account gets the standard 15 days.
- **Membership doesn't help with phones.** Activatable devices stay at 14 days for members too.
- **Marketplace items don't get the member extension.** Products shipped from or sold by third-party Marketplace Sellers follow the standard return policy unless the listing says otherwise.

## Is there a holiday return window?

Yes. Best Buy's [Return & Exchange Policy](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/help-topics/return-exchange-policy/pcmcat260800050014.c?id=pcmcat260800050014) lists **extended holiday return and exchange periods** for 2026: purchases made **Oct. 30 through Dec. 31, 2026** have an extended return period **through Jan. 15, 2027**.

| Purchase | Last day of return window |
| --- | --- |
| Most Best Buy purchases (Oct. 30 to Dec. 31, 2026) | January 15, 2027 |
| My Best Buy Plus and Total member purchases made Oct. 30 to Dec. 3, 2026 | January 31, 2027 |
| My Best Buy Plus and Total member purchases made on or after Dec. 4, 2026 | 60 days |
| Activatable devices (cell phones, cellular tablets, mobile hotspots, cellular wearables) | 14 days (30 days for Verizon activatable devices) |
| Holiday decorations (artificial trees, lights, decorations) | 15 days |

Best Buy counts the day you get the product as day 1. The holiday extension **doesn't apply** to items bought with a third-party contract, such as activatable devices and AppleCare+ plans, or to holiday decorations. Best Buy says restocking fees, nonreturnable products and the rest of its return policy still apply.

## Restocking fees

Best Buy charges a restocking fee on some returned items:

| Product | Restocking fee |
| --- | --- |
| Activatable devices (excluding prepaid phones) | $45 |
| Drones, digital cameras, camera lenses, camcorders (including action cameras), golf launch monitors, premium scooters, super scooters, mobility scooters, electric wheelchairs, leg and body recovery systems, projectors, projector screens, premium cold tubs, premium saunas and special-order products | 15% of the purchase price |

**When there's no restocking fee:** Best Buy says there's no fee if the product is **unopened**, or if you bought it in Alabama, Colorado, Hawaii, Iowa, Mississippi, Ohio, Oklahoma or South Carolina, or where the law prohibits it. In some states the restocking fee is taxed.

## Can you return opened items?

Yes, most opened items can be returned within the window, but they need to be in **like-new condition** with all accessories and packaging. Best Buy may deny a return for items that are damaged, unsanitary, dented, scratched or missing major contents. If accessories or packaging are missing, Best Buy may refuse the return or take a nonrefundable deduction from your refund. Apparel must be unworn, unwashed and still have its tags.

Some items can't be returned once opened:

- Products that interact with bodily fluids
- SIM kits
- LEGO products
- Consumables, including batteries, cleaning agents, filters, health supplements, health test kits, ink and 3D printer filament
- Completed services, and delivered home standby generators or whole-home backup systems

Opened physical copies of software, movies, music, video games and books bought from Best Buy can **only be exchanged for the identical item**. From Marketplace Sellers, they're nonreturnable once opened.

## Items you can't return at all

Best Buy lists these as nonreturnable once purchased:

- Custom or personalized orders and items marked **Final Sale**
- Non-subscription digital content, including digital games (Microsoft Office is the exception)
- Codes and prepaid cards, including third-party gift cards and prepaid phone cards
- Memberships, trading cards (including Pokémon cards) and vehicle replacement key fobs
- Products with a capacity of 300 watt-hours or more, golf carts, utility terrain vehicles, electric bikes, electric mopeds and plumbing items

For services and subscriptions (such as antivirus software and Microsoft 365), Best Buy points to the terms at BestBuy.com/PlanTerms.

## Returning without a receipt

Best Buy's policy says returns **without proof of purchase** may require an email address, will be refunded as **store credit**, may be denied or limited, and **won't include sales tax and fees**. So a receipt (or the order in your Best Buy account) makes a big difference.

## How to return something to Best Buy

1. **Gather everything:** the item, all original accessories and the packaging.
2. **Wipe connected devices.** Remove your data and unlink the device from any cloud account or other device, or Best Buy may not accept it.
3. **Choose a return method:**
   - **In store:** return any in-store or online Best Buy purchase at any Best Buy store. Bring your receipt or packing slip and the card you paid with for faster processing.
   - **By mail:** log in to your Best Buy account, find the order, print the free prepaid label and drop it off during your return window. Major appliances, TVs over 50 inches, kegerators and SodaStream makers can't be shipped back.
4. **Major appliances and truck deliveries:** call 1-888-BEST BUY (1-888-237-8289) instead of going to a store.
5. **Phones with a carrier contract:** you're responsible for canceling service with the carrier and any carrier charges. Tell the store associate you want to cancel, or call Best Buy Mobile at 1-877-702-2211.

**Refunds** usually go back to your original payment method. Mail returns are processed within 7 to 10 business days of arriving, and your bank may take up to 10 more days. If you paid more than $800 in cash, or more than $250 by check or by debit card without a major credit card logo, Best Buy refunds by check within 10 business days. Gift card payments come back as a Best Buy gift card.

## How to never miss the Best Buy return window

Fifteen days goes fast. A few habits help:

- **Keep the receipt or packing slip** until the window has closed, and keep the original box and accessories with it.
- **Take a photo of the receipt** the day you buy. Paper receipts get lost and fade.
- **Write down the real deadline.** Count from the day you *received* the item, and use the right window: 15, 60, 14 or 30 days.
- **Set a reminder a few days early**, so you have time to test the item and get to a store.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many days do you have to return something to Best Buy?

Most products: 15 days from the day you receive the item. My Best Buy Plus and Total members get 60 days on most products. Activatable devices like phones get 14 days for everyone, and Verizon activatable devices get 30.

### Can I return something to Best Buy without a receipt?

Possibly, but it's not guaranteed. Best Buy says no-receipt returns may require an email address, are refunded as store credit, may be denied or limited, and don't refund sales tax and fees.

### Does Best Buy charge a restocking fee on opened phones?

Yes. Opened activatable devices (excluding prepaid phones) have a $45 restocking fee. There's no fee if the device is unopened, or if you bought it in AL, CO, HI, IA, MS, OH, OK or SC, or where the law prohibits it.

### Does the free My Best Buy account give you 60 days?

No. The 60-day window is for the paid My Best Buy Plus and My Best Buy Total memberships. The free account gets the standard 15 days.

### Does Best Buy extend returns for the holidays in 2026?

Yes. Most Best Buy purchases made Oct. 30 through Dec. 31, 2026 can be returned through January 15, 2027. My Best Buy Plus and Total members get until January 31, 2027 for purchases made Oct. 30 through Dec. 3, 2026, and 60 days for purchases on or after Dec. 4. Activatable devices (14 days, or 30 for Verizon), other items bought with a third-party contract such as AppleCare+, and holiday decorations (15 days) are excluded.

### Can I return an online Best Buy order to a store?

Yes. Best Buy says you can return any in-store or online purchase to any Best Buy store. Many Marketplace items can also be returned in store, with some size and product limits.
