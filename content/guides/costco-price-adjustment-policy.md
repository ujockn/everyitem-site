Costco's Black Friday deals for 2026 are valid only from **Nov. 27 to Nov. 30**, according to its [Holiday Savings page](https://www.costco.com/o/-/holiday-savings), and Costco says a price adjustment request made after a promotion ends may be denied. So if something you bought earlier this month shows up in that sale, you have four days, not 30, to get the difference back. Here's how Costco's price adjustment policy works, step by step, for warehouse and Costco.com purchases. Every rule below comes from Costco's own help pages, linked in [Sources](#sources).

**Last checked: Nov 23, 2026.** Every Item is an independent guide and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Costco or Citi. Costco says it may restrict this policy and can deny any request, so confirm on the official page before you rely on a date.

## Costco's price adjustment rules at a glance

Costco calls it a "price match" on its [help page](https://customerservice.costco.com/app/answers/answer_view/a_id/628), but it only covers Costco's own prices dropping after you buy, which most shoppers call a price adjustment. The page was last revised Nov. 20, 2026.

| Question | What Costco says |
| --- | --- |
| How long do I have? | 30 days from the date of purchase |
| Sale-dated promotions | You must ask within the promotion dates **and** within 30 days of buying |
| Which lower prices count? | Costco's own. Costco doesn't match other businesses or retailers |
| Online vs warehouse | Costco.com orders aren't matched to warehouse prices |
| Where to ask | Warehouse buys: the Returns counter where you bought. Costco.com orders: the online form |
| How you're paid (online) | Credit to your original form of payment, typically 5 to 10 business days after approval |
| Not eligible | Precious metals. Resellers only get promotional prices bought during the promotion |
| Quantity limits | If a promotion has an item limit, only that many units qualify |

## How to ask for a warehouse price adjustment

1. **Go back to the same warehouse.** Costco says to ask at the Returns counter of the Costco location where you made the purchase. Costco.com customer service can't process price adjustments for in-warehouse purchases.
2. **Bring your receipt and membership card.** Costco's page doesn't list what proof it wants, so it helps to also note the item number and the new price.
3. **Ask before the deal ends** if the lower price is a dated promotion.

Costco's page doesn't say whether a warehouse adjustment comes back as a card credit or cash, so ask at the counter.

If you lost the paper receipt, Costco shows up to two years of in-warehouse receipts online. Sign in at Costco.com, go to **Orders & Returns**, then the **In-Warehouse** tab, according to [Costco's receipts help page](https://customerservice.costco.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/10306). Your membership must be verified and linked to the account, and a new purchase can take up to 24 hours to appear.

## How to ask for a Costco.com price adjustment

1. **Check your order first.** Costco suggests confirming the promotional price wasn't already taken off your order.
2. **Open the [Costco price match page](https://customerservice.costco.com/app/answers/answer_view/a_id/628)** and select **Request a Price Match**, then fill in the form.
3. **Wait for approval.** Once approved, Costco says credits typically reach your original form of payment within 5 to 10 business days.

One catch: you can't use a lower **warehouse** price for an online order. Costco says its online prices include shipping and handling that don't apply in the warehouse, and its [online vs warehouse prices page](https://customerservice.costco.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/691) adds that warehouse-only promotions can make prices differ.

## Black Friday and Holiday Savings: the promotion-date rule

This is the rule that trips people up in November. For items with specific sale dates, such as Holiday Savings promotions, Costco wants the request **within both the promotion dates and 30 days of purchase**, and may deny requests made after the promotion ends.

Costco's [Holiday Savings page](https://www.costco.com/o/-/holiday-savings) says this year's savings run Oct. 30 to Nov. 30, 2026, with dates that vary by item. The Black Friday group is valid Nov. 27 to Nov. 30, and the whole event ends Nov. 30.

**Example (dates only):** you buy a vacuum at the warehouse on Nov. 10. Your 30 days run to about Dec. 10. But if that vacuum is in the Nov. 27 to 30 Black Friday deals, ask by **Nov. 30**, while the promotion is still running, or Costco may say no. Costco doesn't spell out how it counts the 30 days, so don't cut it close.

Two more holiday details from Costco's price match page:

- **Item limits still apply.** If the deal says "Limit 5," only up to 5 units qualify for an adjustment.
- **The $50 off $500 Costco.com offer** doesn't apply to items bought before Nov. 16, 2026.

The Holiday Savings page shows warehouse pricing and says online pricing may differ. A warehouse deal won't lower a Costco.com order.

## What Costco won't adjust

- **Other stores' prices.** Costco doesn't match Walmart, Amazon or anyone else. If you want a competitor's price matched, see which stores do in our [price match guarantee guide](/guides/price-match-guarantee/).
- **Warehouse prices on Costco.com orders**, as above.
- **Precious metals.** Costco says they aren't eligible for price matches or promotions (and its [return policy](https://customerservice.costco.com/app/answers/answer_view/a_id/1191) lists them as non-refundable).
- **Resale purchases.** Eligibility is limited to non-resale members and products. Resellers have to buy during the promotion to get the promotional price.

Two categories have their own rules. **Tires and wheels** qualify if all the other conditions are met (online buys use the form, warehouse buys go to the warehouse). **Special Order Kiosk and installed home improvement products** follow the contract date and pre-tax price, with Shop Card exceptions limited to 14 days before and after the official promotion dates, so contact the authorized dealer.

## Watching for a price drop

Costco's help pages don't mention price drop alerts, so it's up to you to notice. Its [inventory and prices page](https://customerservice.costco.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/1015066/) says the Costco app shows warehouse inventory, and customer service chat can look up a warehouse's price by item number. Costco notes that this pricing can be delayed by up to 30 minutes.

## Missed the 30 days?

A price adjustment is off the table after 30 days, but you still have options:

- **Return it under the satisfaction guarantee.** [Costco's return policy](https://customerservice.costco.com/app/answers/answer_view/a_id/1191) promises a refund on most products with no deadline listed, though "limitations apply." Some members return the item and buy it again at the lower price. Costco doesn't describe that as a price adjustment, so expect the warehouse to make the call.
- **Electronics have 90 days.** TVs, computers, tablets, phones, cameras and major appliances must be returned within 90 days of when you received them, so a return-and-rebuy only works inside that window. Our [Costco return policy guide](/guides/costco-return-policy/) has the full list.
- **Don't count on the Costco Visa.** [Citi's Costco Anywhere Visa page](https://www.citi.com/credit-cards/citi-costco-anywhere-visa-credit-card) lists Damage & Theft Purchase Protection (120 days, 90 for New York residents) but no price protection benefit.

For gifts and other late returns, see [what you can still do after a return window closes](/guides/missed-return-window/) and our [2026 holiday return deadlines](/guides/holiday-return-deadlines-2026/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How many days does Costco give for a price adjustment?

30 days from the date of purchase, for Costco's own price drops. If the lower price is a dated promotion, you also have to ask before the promotion ends.

### Does Costco price adjust Black Friday deals?

Yes, if you bought the item within the last 30 days and you ask while the deal is running. Costco's 2026 Black Friday deals are valid Nov. 27 to Nov. 30, and Costco says requests after a promotion ends may be denied.

### Does Costco price match other stores?

No. Costco says it doesn't price match other businesses or retailers. It only adjusts for its own lower price.

### Can I get a price adjustment at any Costco warehouse?

Costco says to ask at the Returns counter of the warehouse where you made the purchase. Costco.com can't handle adjustments for warehouse purchases.

### Will Costco.com match the lower warehouse price?

No. Costco doesn't match warehouse prices for Costco.com purchases, because online prices include shipping and handling.

### How does Costco pay a price adjustment?

For Costco.com orders, approved credits typically go to your original form of payment within 5 to 10 business days. Costco's page doesn't say how warehouse adjustments are paid, so ask at the Returns counter.

### What if it's been more than 30 days?

You can't get a price adjustment, but most products can still be returned under Costco's satisfaction guarantee (electronics and major appliances within 90 days of receipt). Rebuying at the lower price is up to you and the warehouse.
