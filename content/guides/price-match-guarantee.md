Finding the same TV or drill for less somewhere else only saves you money if the store you're standing in has promised to match it, and fewer stores do than many shoppers think. Target's official policy now lists only Target prices as match sources, Walmart stores match only Walmart.com, and Costco and Amazon say they don't match other retailers. The big chains that still match a competitor are **Best Buy, The Home Depot, Lowe's and Staples stores**. This guide covers which competitors each one accepts, what is excluded, what proof to bring and how to ask, at checkout and after you buy. Every detail comes from official store pages, linked in [Sources](#sources).

**Last checked: Nov 11, 2026.** Every Item is an independent guide and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Best Buy, The Home Depot, Lowe's, Staples, Target, Walmart, Costco or Amazon. Stores can change these rules at any time, so confirm on the official page before you rely on a match.

## Price match or price adjustment?

- **Price match (this guide):** a store agrees to sell you an item at a lower price you found, usually a competitor's, typically when you check out.
- **Price adjustment:** you already bought the item, the **same store** later lowers its own price, and you ask for the difference back. See our [price adjustment policies guide](/guides/price-adjustment-policies/) for Target, Best Buy, Costco and Home Depot windows.

Many stores call both of these a "price match," so read the details. Target's "Price Match Guarantee," for example, is about Target's own prices, not a competitor's.

## Best Buy

Best Buy's [Price Match Guarantee](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/help-topics/price-match-guarantee/pcmcat290300050002.c?id=pcmcat290300050002) matches the current price of its **Qualified Competitors** at the time of sale, for new items sold by Best Buy that are immediately available. The [official competitor list](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/price-match-guarantee/qualified-competitors/pcmcat1693426756861.c?id=pcmcat1693426756861) has 19 names: Abt, Amazon, Apple, B&H Photo Video, BJ's Wholesale Club, BrandsMart USA, Costco, Crutchfield, Dick's Sporting Goods, Home Depot, Lowe's, Menards, Microcenter, Nebraska Furniture Mart, P.C. Richard, RC Willey, Sam's Club, Target and Walmart. Stores not on that list aren't matched.

**What has to line up:**

- Same brand, model number and color, and the product must be new.
- The competitor has to have it ready to ship or pick up. Out-of-stock and limited-quantity offers don't count.
- The competitor must sell it itself. Items from third-party Marketplace sellers, on Best Buy or on the competitor's site, are excluded.
- Best Buy matches the pre-tax price only, and shipping and delivery charges are left out of the comparison.

**Warehouse clubs count,** since Costco, BJ's and Sam's Club are on the list. If the club only shows the price after you sign in, Best Buy says the match can only be done in a store, from the live offer, not a screenshot.

**Black Friday and holiday catches.** Best Buy doesn't match competitor prices marked as Black Friday pricing, special daily or hourly sales, or anything on sale at a competitor from the Thursday before Thanksgiving through the Monday after. In 2026 Thanksgiving is Nov. 26, so that's Thursday, Nov. 19 through Monday, Nov. 30. Best Buy's [Holiday Price Match Guarantee](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/price-match-guarantee/best-buy-price-match-guarantee/pcmcat297300050000.c?id=pcmcat297300050000) (purchases Oct. 30 to Dec. 31, 2026, matched through Jan. 15, 2027) only covers Best Buy's own lower price, not a competitor's.

**Other exclusions** include membership and loyalty-only prices, clearance, open-box, refurbished and pre-owned items, bundles, financing, gift card and free-gift offers, coupons, trade-in offers, pre-order offers, contract phones and pricing errors. You can use a coupon or a price match on an item, but not both.

**How to ask:**

- **In a store:** show any employee the competitor's current ad or web page with the lower price. Best Buy may call or check the competitor to verify it.
- **On BestBuy.com or the app (including store pickup):** use Best Buy chat or call 1-888-BEST BUY (1-888-237-8289) before you check out. Best Buy says BestBuy.com price matches can't be done in stores or by email.

**After you buy,** Best Buy's page describes matching **its own** lower price during your return and exchange period, which our [price adjustment guide](/guides/price-adjustment-policies/#best-buy) covers. For return windows, see the [Best Buy return policy guide](/guides/best-buy-return-policy/).

## The Home Depot

The Home Depot's [price match page](https://www.homedepot.com/c/price-match-and-price-check) matches **local stores** in your area and these **online retailers**: Lowe's, Menards, Amazon, Walmart, Best Buy and Target. For Lowe's, Amazon, Walmart, Best Buy and Target, the item has to be sold and fulfilled by that retailer, not a marketplace seller. Home Depot says it doesn't match auction, marketplace or membership-based sites, naming eBay, Wayfair, Costco and Sam's Club.

**What has to line up:** the same brand, model and size, including finish and color. The item must be in stock at both stores, and the competitor has to be able to sell and ship it to where you live.

**How to ask:**

- **In a store:** show the cashier an ad, printout or link with the competitor's lower price at checkout.
- **On homedepot.com:** contact Customer Solutions by live chat or at 1-800-430-3376 with the Home Depot SKU or Internet number, the competitor's name and the competitor's product number.

**After you buy:** you have 30 days from the purchase date to ask for the difference if the price drops at Home Depot or at a competitor it matches. Bring proof of purchase and proof of the lower price.

**What's excluded:** special-order and custom items, clearance and closeout items, marketplace sellers, and limited-time promotions such as Black Friday. Coupons and promo codes can't be combined with a price match.

## Lowe's

Lowe's [Lowest Price Guarantee](https://www.lowes.com/l/about/lowest-price-guarantee) matches a current lower price on an identical, in-stock item from another retailer, and it **counts the competitor's shipping or delivery cost** in the comparison. The competitor has to be able to ship the item to you. Amazon counts only when the item is sold by Amazon.com, not a third-party seller.

**How to ask:**

- **In a store:** show an associate the website, ad, printout or photo so they can check the price.
- **Online or by phone:** chat with Lowe's or call 1-877-465-6937. Lowe's only takes requests from the person who made the purchase.

**After you buy:** Lowe's [returns page](https://www.lowes.com/l/help/returns-policy) says to chat or call if you find a lower price later, and Lowe's will review whether it qualifies. No time limit is published, so ask soon.

**What's excluded:** membership warehouse club prices, marketplace, auction and closeout sites, clearance, seasonal, discontinued, open-box and refurbished items, special orders, installation and labor, rebates and BOGO-style offers, military or loyalty pricing, and one-time promotions like Black Friday and other holiday events. It can't be combined with other Lowe's offers or coupons.

## Staples

Staples' [Price Match Guarantee](https://www.staples.com/sbd/cre/marketing/pmg) is for **purchases in a Staples store only**. At the register, Staples matches a lower price on a new, identical item sold and shipped by Amazon.com (not its Marketplace sellers) or by any retailer that sells both in stores and online under the same name. The item must match down to the model number, components and U.S. warranty, and be in stock from an authorized U.S. seller. It's one match per customer, per item.

If the competitor charges shipping, Staples adds a shipping cost to their price before comparing. Rebates, gift cards and free add-on items from the competitor aren't counted.

**After you buy:** if you bought in a Staples store and find a lower price at Staples or staples.com within 14 days, Staples will refund the difference. That's Staples' own price, not a competitor's.

**What's excluded:** special events, including clearance, timed sales and Black Friday or Cyber Monday sales, plus Tech Services, Print & Marketing, gift cards, postage, In Store Pickup orders and Membership or business contract prices.

## Stores that don't match competitors

**Target.** Target's [price match policy](https://www.target.com/help/article/000062256) only lists Target.com, Target stores and Target Circle deals as match sources, within 14 days of purchase. For holiday 2026, purchases from Nov. 1 qualify if Target's price drops on or before Dec. 24, 2026. Details are in our [price adjustment guide](/guides/price-adjustment-policies/#target) and the [Target return policy guide](/guides/target-return-policy/).

**Walmart.** Walmart's [price match policy](https://corporate.walmart.com/policies) says stores match the identical item's Walmart.com price, if you mention it at checkout, but not competitors' prices. Clearance, Rollback, Black Friday and Cyber Monday deals and Marketplace sellers are excluded, and the store manager decides. Walmart.com doesn't price match at all, including items that dropped after you bought them. See the [Walmart return policy guide](/guides/walmart-return-policy/) if a return is your fallback.

**Costco.** Costco's [price match page](https://customerservice.costco.com/app/answers/answer_view/a_id/628) says it doesn't match other businesses or retailers. It does refund its own price drops within 30 days.

**Amazon.** Amazon's [pricing help page](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=TDZ7rEMCXwi0eZtpmR) says it doesn't offer price matching compensation.

## The catches almost every store shares

- **Identical means identical.** Same brand, model number, size and color. A slightly different model number or bundle won't qualify.
- **Sold by the store itself.** Amazon, Walmart and Best Buy Marketplace sellers are excluded wherever a policy mentions them.
- **In stock now.** Out-of-stock, limited-quantity and pre-order deals are out.
- **Member-only prices are limited.** Best Buy and Lowe's exclude prices only available to members or loyalty programs, and Home Depot and Lowe's don't match membership warehouse clubs.
- **Black Friday week is a gap.** Best Buy, Home Depot, Lowe's and Staples all exclude Black Friday-type deals.
- **No stacking.** Best Buy, Home Depot and Lowe's say you can't combine a match with their coupons.

## How to ask for a price match

1. **Find the live price** on the competitor's own site or ad, sold by that retailer, in stock, and shipping to your area.
2. **Check the model number** on both listings.
3. **Ask before you pay.** Most competitor matches are made at the time of sale. In a store, show the page on your phone. Online, open chat or call before checking out.
4. **Keep the proof** of what you paid. You'll need the receipt or order number for any later adjustment or return.
5. **If you're refused,** ask which exclusion applies. If the store won't match and you've already bought, check its return window and [what to do if you miss it](/guides/missed-return-window/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Which stores still price match competitors?

Among big US chains, Best Buy, The Home Depot, Lowe's and Staples stores publish a guarantee to match a competitor's lower price on an identical, in-stock item. Target, Walmart, Costco and Amazon don't match other retailers.

### Does Target still price match Amazon or Walmart?

Not under its current policy. Target's official price match page only lists Target.com, Target stores and Target Circle deals as sources, so a lower Amazon or Walmart price doesn't qualify.

### Does Walmart price match other stores?

No. Walmart stores will match the price of the identical item on Walmart.com if you mention it at checkout, but Walmart's policy says it doesn't match competitors' online or in-store prices.

### Will Best Buy match Amazon?

Yes, if the item is sold by Amazon itself, not a Marketplace seller, is new and identical, and is available right away. Amazon is one of Best Buy's 19 Qualified Competitors. The match is made at the time of sale, and Black Friday week deals are excluded.

### Can I get a price match after I already bought something?

Usually only for the store's own price. Best Buy, Target, Costco and Staples refund their own later price drops within set windows. Home Depot also covers a matched competitor's lower price within 30 days, and Lowe's says to chat or call and it will review the request.

### Do stores price match Black Friday deals?

Mostly no. Best Buy, Home Depot, Lowe's and Staples all exclude Black Friday-type promotions, and Best Buy doesn't match competitors from the Thursday before Thanksgiving through the following Monday. Walmart stores exclude Black Friday and Cyber Monday deals from Walmart.com matches.

### What proof do I need for a price match?

Show the competitor's current ad or web page with the lower price, ideally live on your phone. Best Buy and Home Depot may check the competitor's site or store themselves. For a member-only warehouse club price, Best Buy needs the live offer in a store, not a screenshot.

### Will stores match a membership or warehouse club price?

It depends. Best Buy matches Costco, BJ's and Sam's Club as listed competitors, but sign-in-only prices must be shown live in a store. Home Depot and Lowe's don't match membership-based warehouse clubs, and Best Buy and Lowe's exclude prices only available to members or loyalty programs.
