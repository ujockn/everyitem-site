Electronics prices often drop right after you buy, and the window to get the difference back can be short. Micro Center's [price protection article](https://community.microcenter.com/kb/articles/631-do-you-offer-price-protection) only refunds a drop that happens inside your return window, and its [return policy](https://www.microcenter.com/site/content/return-policy-page.aspx) gives laptops, desktops, tablets and phones just **15 days**. Dell gives 30 days, Apple 14, and Samsung and B&H tie it to their return windows. This guide covers what each store's official page says about refunding a price drop after you buy, the Black Friday catches, what you need and how to ask. Every detail comes from official store pages, linked in [Sources](#sources).

**Last checked: Nov 18, 2026.** Every Item is an independent guide and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Dell, Apple, Samsung, B&H Photo, Micro Center or Newegg. Stores can change these rules at any time, so confirm on the official page before you rely on a date.

## Price adjustment or price match?

- **Price adjustment (also called price protection or a price guarantee):** you already bought the item, the same store later lowers its price, and you ask for the difference back.
- **Price match:** the store matches a lower price from another store. Usually that's at checkout, but Dell, Samsung and Newegg also do it after you buy.

## Dell

Dell's [Price Guarantee and Competitor Price Match page](https://www.dell.com/en-us/shop/dell-experience/lp/price-match-guarantee) has the longest window here. Under its **30-Day Price Guarantee**, if the identical product shows a lower price on Dell.com within 30 days of your invoice date, Dell refunds the difference. It only covers purchases made on Dell.com.

**Holiday 2026.** Dell says PCs, monitors and accessories bought on dell.com/en-us from **Nov. 1 to Dec. 29, 2026** get an extended Price Guarantee period until **Jan. 15, 2027**, or 30 days from the invoice date, whichever is later. So a laptop bought on Black Friday can be checked against Dell's own prices through mid-January.

**Competitor prices after you buy.** Dell will also refund the difference if a listed retailer, such as Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart, Target, Apple, HP or Lenovo, sells the identical product for less within 30 days of your invoice. For personal computers, a competitor match is capped at **10% of the Dell price**. Dell excludes doorbuster, flash, deal-of-the-day and special holiday prices from other stores, and prices from membership clubs such as Costco and Sam's Club.

**The limits:**

- One approved pricing credit per product: either the Price Guarantee or the competitor match, not both.
- Five refunds per customer, and a $10,000 maximum purchase amount.
- Excluded: marketplace resellers, financing and gift card offers, bundles with free items, pricing errors, limited-quantity, out-of-stock, clearance and Outlet products, Deal of the Week, Daily Deals, coupons and rebates.
- Personal-use purchases only. Business accounts, resellers and bulk buys don't qualify.

**How to ask:** save the link to the lower price. Then go to Dell's Order Support portal and choose Get Help, then My Refunds, then Create Service Request. For competitor prices, choose Better Competitor Pricing. A customer care agent checks the request before refunding.

## Apple

Apple's [U.S. Sales and Refund Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/sales-support/sales-policies/retail_us.html) says that if Apple lowers the price of an **Apple-branded product within 14 calendar days of the date you receive it**, you can ask for a refund or credit of the difference. Ask at an Apple Store or by calling **1-800-MY-APPLE**, within 14 days of the price change.

The catch for holiday shoppers: it **excludes limited-time price reductions and special sales events, such as Black Friday**. A promotion during an Apple shopping event won't get you money back on something you already bought. Price protection is also limited to 10 units of a product, and Apple may ask to see the product or proof that you still have it.

If Apple simply lowers the regular price, for example when a new model launches, that's worth asking about. If the price change is a promotion, the only route the policy leaves open is a return within Apple's return window and a new purchase.

## Samsung

Samsung's US help article [Does Samsung offer price matching?](https://order-help.us.samsung.com/articles/promotions/does-samsung-offer-price-matching/669e317579828c791e799f18) treats a price drop as a price match. You can ask on an order that **hasn't been delivered or picked up yet, or that is still within its 15-day return window**. If it's approved after you order, you get a refund of the difference. Samsung says it answers within 24 hours.

**What you can match depends on the product:**

- **Phones, tablets, laptops and wearables** (watches, Buds, Ring, XR): only against Samsung.com and the Shop Samsung App. So if your Galaxy tablet gets cheaper on Samsung.com within the window, you can ask for the difference. A lower price at another store doesn't count.
- **TVs, projectors, speakers and soundbars, monitors, and memory and storage:** against a list of named retailers on Samsung's page, such as Amazon, Best Buy and B&H Photo. The item has to be sold and shipped by that retailer. Membership stores such as Costco and Sam's Club don't count.

**The limits:** the lower price has to be advertised and in stock when you ask. It's compared without tax, shipping, open-box or used pricing, and without trade-in or mail-in offers. Samsung only approves one Samsung.com or app price match per order. To ask, contact Samsung's Order Support team with your order ID, the product name, the retailer, the listing's link and a screenshot of it.

Samsung's [return policy](https://order-help.us.samsung.com/articles/returns-refunds/what-is-samsungs-return-policy/669e3173cd7d5a05a5318406) counts the 15 days **from delivery**, and your exact deadline is shown on the My Orders page.

## B&H Photo

B&H's [Price Match Guarantee page](https://www.bhphotovideo.com/find/HelpCenter/priceMatch.jsp) has a section on prior purchases. If the price of an item you bought is reduced and you're still within the **return and exchange period**, B&H says it **may** honor the lower price and refund the difference to your original payment method. It's a "may," not a promise.

B&H's [return policy](https://www.bhphotovideo.com/find/HelpCenter/ReturnExchange.jsp) gives **30 days from the delivery date** for most items. For the holidays, items bought after **Nov. 1, 2026 through Jan. 1, 2027** can be returned or exchanged until **Feb. 1, 2027**. B&H doesn't say whether that holiday extension also stretches the price adjustment window, so ask early if you can.

**How to ask:** contact B&H Customer Service by chat, email or phone at 800-221-5743 during business hours, with your order details. Price matches on new purchases go through a different team, the Product Specialists.

## Micro Center

Micro Center's [price protection article](https://community.microcenter.com/kb/articles/631-do-you-offer-price-protection) says it refunds the difference if **Micro Center lowers the price within your item's return window**. Its [return policy](https://www.microcenter.com/site/content/return-policy-page.aspx) counts that window from the purchase date:

- **15 calendar days:** desktops, laptops, tablets, processors, motherboards, drones, microphones, digital and video cameras, projectors, cell phones and 3D printers.
- **30 calendar days:** all other products. Your item's own return period is shown on its product page under Support Resources.
- **Trading card games** only get price protection for 15 days. They can't be returned at all.

**Black Friday:** price protection doesn't cover **other retailers'** special sales such as Black Friday and Cyber Monday. It's about Micro Center's own price. It also won't match a new item to an open-box or clearance listing.

**How to ask:**

- **Store purchases paid by Visa, Mastercard or Discover card:** go back to the store with your purchase information and **the same card** you paid with.
- **Cash, check or a Micro Center gift card:** go back to the store with your purchase information.
- **Paid only with a Micro Center Insider (Wells Fargo) credit card:** you can use Micro Center's support chat for one remote refund. Later requests have to be made in the store with the card.
- **Motherboard, CPU and RAM bundles:** in the store only, with your receipt.
- **Online (shipped) orders:** use Micro Center's support chat.

## Newegg

Newegg's [Price Match Guarantee](https://promotions.newegg.com/nepro/20-2459/index.html) only applies to items that showed a **Price Match Guarantee badge** when you bought them. If Newegg or a listed major retailer (such as Amazon, Best Buy, Dell, Target or Walmart) advertises the same brand and model for less, you have **14 calendar days** from your purchase date to submit a claim form.

**The catches:**

- The difference comes back as a **Newegg Customer Care card**, good on Newegg.com or the Newegg app for 90 days, not as cash.
- It's **not valid on major holiday sale prices**, and Newegg names Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Green Monday and its FantasTech sale.
- Excluded: open-box and refurbished items, clearance, bundles, rebate and coupon prices, out-of-stock or limited-quantity items, prices that run less than 6 hours a day, and items with MAP pricing.
- One price match per customer and one per item.

Newegg sometimes runs separate event programs, such as its fall 2026 [FantasTech Price Protection](https://www.newegg.com/promotions/fantastech/26-0850/index.html), which automatically refunded the difference on badged items bought Sept. 28 to Oct. 4 if Newegg.com lowered the price by Oct. 11. Check the product page for a badge before you buy.

## Bought on Black Friday? What to expect

If the price drops again after you buy on Black Friday:

- **Dell:** the most room. Its own lower price counts for 30 days, and holiday purchases get until Jan. 15, 2027. Other stores' doorbuster and holiday prices don't count for a competitor match.
- **Apple:** a permanent price cut counts. A special sales event like Black Friday doesn't.
- **Micro Center:** a lower Micro Center price counts within your return window, but another store's Black Friday price doesn't.
- **Samsung and B&H:** neither page we checked mentions a Black Friday exclusion. Both tie it to the return window, so ask before the window closes.
- **Newegg:** Black Friday and Cyber Monday prices are excluded.

## How to ask for a price adjustment

1. **Find your start date.** Dell counts from the invoice date, Apple, Samsung and B&H from delivery, and Micro Center from the purchase date.
2. **Save the lower price as proof.** Dell wants the link, and Samsung wants a link and a screenshot. Prices can change back within hours.
3. **Have your order number or receipt ready.** Micro Center store purchases also need the card you paid with.
4. **Use the right channel.** Dell uses its Order Support portal, Apple a store or 1-800-MY-APPLE, Samsung its Order Support team, B&H Customer Service, Micro Center the store or chat, and Newegg a claim form.
5. **Ask once, clearly.** Dell, Samsung and Newegg limit how many requests they approve, so make sure the price is the one you want before you ask.

## If the window closed: return and rebuy

If you're past the adjustment window but still within the return window, some shoppers return the item and buy it again at the lower price. That's not a store promise, just a workaround. It only works if the item is still returnable, and some stores charge restocking fees on opened electronics: B&H can charge at least 15% if a return doesn't meet its conditions, and Samsung charges up to 15% on some products. Check the dates in our [holiday return deadlines](/guides/holiday-return-deadlines-2026/) guide, and if the return date has also passed, see [what you can still do after a return window closes](/guides/missed-return-window/).

Every store above asks for an order number, invoice or receipt, and the clock starts on the day you buy or receive the item. ## Frequently asked questions

### Do electronics stores give price adjustments if an item drops after Black Friday?

Some do. Dell refunds a lower Dell.com price within 30 days of your invoice, and holiday purchases get until Jan. 15, 2027. Micro Center, Samsung and B&H tie it to your return window. Apple excludes Black Friday and special sales events, and Newegg excludes Black Friday and Cyber Monday prices.

### What happens if a tablet price drops after I buy it?

It depends on where you bought it. Apple refunds the difference on its own permanent price cuts within 14 days of receiving the iPad. Samsung matches a lower Samsung.com or Shop Samsung App price within the 15-day return window. Micro Center gives 15 days on tablets. For Best Buy, Target and Amazon, see our [price adjustment policies guide](/guides/price-adjustment-policies/).

### Does Apple give a price adjustment after an Apple shopping event?

No. Apple's price protection excludes limited-time price reductions and special sales events, such as Black Friday. It covers Apple lowering the price of an Apple-branded product within 14 calendar days of when you receive it.

### How long does Dell's price guarantee last?

30 days from your invoice date, for purchases on Dell.com. PCs, monitors and accessories bought on dell.com/en-us from Nov. 1 to Dec. 29, 2026 get until Jan. 15, 2027, or 30 days from the invoice, whichever is later.

### Does Samsung refund the difference if the price drops?

Yes, through its price match. If your order hasn't been delivered yet, or is still in its 15-day return window, Samsung can refund the difference. For phones, tablets, laptops and wearables, only a lower Samsung.com or Shop Samsung App price counts.

### Does Micro Center do price adjustments?

Yes. Micro Center calls it price protection. It refunds the difference if Micro Center lowers the price within your item's return window: 15 days for laptops, desktops, tablets, CPUs, cameras and phones, and 30 days for most other products.

### Does Newegg refund the difference if the price drops?

Only for items that showed a Price Match Guarantee badge when you bought them, within 14 days of purchase. You get a Newegg Customer Care card instead of cash, and Black Friday and Cyber Monday prices don't count.

### Which electronics store gives the longest price adjustment window?

Of the stores in this guide, Dell, at 30 days from the invoice date, extended to Jan. 15, 2027 for holiday purchases. Micro Center and B&H can also reach 30 days for many items, because they follow the return window.
