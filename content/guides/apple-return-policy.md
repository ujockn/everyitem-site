Apple gives you **14 calendar days** from the day a product arrives to send it back, according to its [Returns & Refunds page](https://www.apple.com/shop/help/returns_refund). Amazon gives most items 30 days and Target gives most items 90, so Apple's clock runs out much sooner. And if the price drops during a Black Friday event, Apple's price protection won't refund the difference, per its [U.S. Sales and Refund Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/sales-support/sales-policies/retail_us.html). The good news for this season: most things you get from Apple's online store between now and Christmas can go back until **Jan. 8, 2027**. This guide covers the window, the holiday dates, price drops, gift returns, refunds and what to do before you hand a device back. Every detail comes from Apple's official pages, linked in [Sources](#sources).

## How long do you have to return to Apple?

Apple's [U.S. Sales and Refund Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/sales-support/sales-policies/retail_us.html) gives you **14 days from the date you receive the product** to return or exchange it with a receipt. That covers Apple Store locations, Apple Store Online, the Apple Store app and orders by phone.

The clock starts on delivery, not on the order date. A MacBook that arrives on a Tuesday is due back two weeks later, on a Tuesday. If you picked it up in a store, the clock starts that day.

The product has to come back in its **original condition**, with every cable, adapter, document and the packaging it came in. Apple says returned products may need an inspection before the refund is approved, and it can take up to 10 business days.

## Apple's 2026 holiday return window

Apple's [Returns & Refunds page](https://www.apple.com/shop/help/returns_refund) says eligible products from the **Apple Store Online** that are **received between Nov. 6 and Dec. 25, 2026** can be returned **through Jan. 8, 2027**.

A few details decide whether you get the extra time:

- **It's the delivery date that counts.** A gift ordered in December that arrives on Dec. 27 gets the normal 14 days, not Jan. 8.
- **Carrier-financed devices are left out.** iPhones financed through T-Mobile or Verizon, and iPads financed through Verizon, stay on the standard 14 days.
- **Everything else in the policy still applies,** so the item still needs to come back complete and in original condition.
- **Purchases after Dec. 25** fall back to the standard 14 days.

Apple's holiday wording is about the online store. If you bought in an Apple Store location, check your receipt or ask a Specialist before you count on the January date.

## What you can't return to Apple

Apple's Returns & Refunds page lists these as ineligible:

- **Apple Gift Cards**, including older Apple Store and App Store & iTunes cards (see [can you return a gift card](/guides/return-gift-card/) for other stores)
- **Opened software**, unless it hasn't been installed on any computer, and boxed software with a broken license seal
- **Electronic software downloads** and software upgrade products
- **Apple Developer products**, such as memberships and WWDC tickets

Two more limits worth knowing:

- **Engraved items and custom-configured Macs** can't be exchanged in a store. You can still return them for a refund to your original payment method.
- **Products can only go back in the country where you bought them,** and ten or more of the same product must go back to the store where you bought them.

## Price drops: Apple's 14-day price protection

This is the question behind a lot of holiday searches: if Apple cuts the price after you buy, do you get the difference back?

Sometimes. Apple's [Sales and Refund Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/sales-support/sales-policies/retail_us.html) says that if Apple reduces the price of an **Apple-branded product within 14 calendar days of the date you receive it**, you can visit an Apple Store or call **1-800-MY-APPLE** within 14 days of the price change and ask for a refund or credit of the difference. The limits:

- It **excludes limited-time price reductions and special sales events, such as Black Friday.** A shopping-event promotion won't get you money back on something you already bought.
- It's limited to **10 units** of a product, and Apple may ask you to bring the product or show you still have it.
- It only covers **Apple-branded products** sold by Apple.

If the price drop is a permanent one, such as a lower price after a new model launches, ask. If it's a holiday promotion, a return inside the 14 days (or the holiday window) and a new purchase is the only route the policy leaves open. ## How to return something to Apple

**By mail (online orders):**

1. Sign in to your **Order Listing** page on apple.com with your Apple Account.
2. Open the order, choose **Start a Return**, then **Get Started**.
3. Pick the items and select **Initiate Your Return**.
4. Print the shipping label (and any hazmat label), pack the item with everything it came with, and schedule a carrier pickup or drop it off.

**In a store:** take it to any U.S. Apple Store. Apple says the refund is processed at that time. You can also exchange there, if the item you want is in stock.

**A gift:** use Apple's Gift Returns page with the product's **serial number**, or the **order number plus the UPC** from the box. Apple refunds gift returns as an **electronic Apple Gift Card**, not to the giver's card. For gifts from other stores, see [returning a gift without a receipt](/guides/return-gift-without-receipt/).

**Arrived damaged or wrong?** Apple says online orders that arrive damaged or incorrect can go back to any U.S. Apple Store, or you can call 1-800-676-2775.

## How Apple refunds you, and how fast

From Apple's Returns & Refunds page and Sales and Refund Policy:

- **Credit or debit card:** Apple sends the refund to your bank within **five business days** of receiving the return. When it shows up is up to your bank.
- **Apple Gift Card:** refunded as an Apple Gift Card by email.
- **Apple Account balance:** back to your balance, or an Apple Gift Card if the balance is at or near its limit.
- **Cash over $750 in a store:** Apple mails a check within 10 business days.
- **Gift receipt:** an Apple Gift Card.

## Before you hand back an iPhone, iPad or Mac

- **Turn off Find My and erase it.** Apple's policy says it may refuse a return if security features are on and can't be turned off. Apple's support article on [what to do before you give away an iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109511) says erasing the device with Erase All Content and Settings turns off Find My and Activation Lock. Back up first.
- **Call your carrier.** Returning an iPhone or iPad doesn't automatically cancel your wireless service, and Apple says any carrier fees are your responsibility.
- **Deal with AppleCare+.** If you return the AppleCare+ part of an iPhone Upgrade Program purchase, you lose your upgrade option. If you're keeping the device, Apple says you can add [AppleCare+ within 60 days](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104941) of buying a new one.

## Apple products bought somewhere else

Apple's policy is clear: products bought from other retailers go back to that retailer, under its rules. Those windows are often different:

- **Target:** Apple and Beats products get 14 days, and the holiday extension for them ends Jan. 8, 2027. See our [Target return policy guide](/guides/target-return-policy/).
- **Amazon:** Apple-brand products in new condition get 15 days, and Apple-branded products bought Nov. 1 to Dec. 31, 2026 can go back through Jan. 15, 2027. See our [Amazon return policy guide](/guides/amazon-return-policy/).
- **Best Buy:** the standard window is 15 days (longer for paid members), with fees on some opened items. See our [Best Buy return policy guide](/guides/best-buy-return-policy/).

Opened AirPods, iPads and other gadgets have their own quirks at each store; our [electronics return policy guide](/guides/electronics-return-policy/) goes item by item.

## Don't lose track of the 14 days

Fourteen days goes fast, especially with gifts and holiday deliveries. Write down the delivery date, not the order date, and keep the order confirmation. If you already missed it, our [missed return window guide](/guides/missed-return-window/) covers what you can still try.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many days do you have to return something to Apple?

14 calendar days from the day you receive it, for purchases from an Apple Store, Apple Store Online, the Apple Store app or by phone. It has to come back in original condition with everything that was in the box.

### What is Apple's holiday return policy for 2026?

Eligible products from the Apple Store Online received between Nov. 6 and Dec. 25, 2026 can be returned through Jan. 8, 2027. iPhones financed through T-Mobile or Verizon and iPads financed through Verizon stay on the standard 14 days.

### Does Apple refund the difference if the price drops after Black Friday?

Not for Black Friday or other special sales events. Apple's price protection covers other price reductions on Apple-branded products within 14 days of receipt, if you ask within 14 days of the price change, and it excludes limited-time and special-event discounts.

### Can you return opened AirPods or an opened iPhone to Apple?

Yes, within the 14 days (or the holiday window), as long as it's in its original condition with all its parts, accessories and packaging. Apple doesn't list a restocking fee in its U.S. policy. Turn off Find My first, or Apple may refuse the return.

### Does Apple charge a restocking fee?

Apple's U.S. Returns & Refunds page and Sales and Refund Policy don't list one. They do require the product to be in original condition, and returns may be inspected before the refund is approved.

### How do I return an Apple gift without a receipt?

Use Apple's Gift Returns page with the serial number, or the order number plus the UPC from the box. You can also take it to an Apple Store. The refund comes as an electronic Apple Gift Card.

### Can I return an Apple product bought at Target, Amazon or Best Buy to Apple?

No. Apple says products bought from other retailers go back to that retailer under its own policy. Target gives Apple products 14 days, Amazon 15 days for Apple-brand items in new condition, and Best Buy 15 days for most purchases.

### How long does an Apple refund take?

Apple says card refunds go to your bank within five business days of receiving the return; your bank decides when it posts. Returns that need inspection can take up to 10 business days, and cash returns over $750 are refunded by check.
